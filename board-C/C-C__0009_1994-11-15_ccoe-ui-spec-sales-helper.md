## 觀景 / vista

This document describes the user interface for ССОЕ Mass
Market Consultative Sales Helper for Small Business Group (SBG) and Home and Personal Services (H&PS). The system
is designed to meet the user requirements of CCOE dated
March 1, 1994. It will have to be modified to take into account
new Future Mode of Operation (FMO) requirements. The document also describes how Sales and Service Consultants
(SSC) use input devices such as keyboards, a mouse, and screens


---

## 亂語 / text

The purpose of this document is to translate the Mass Market Customer
Contact Operating Environment (CCOE) Consultative Sales requirements,
as defined in the CCOE user requirements document, into user interface
specifications.
The design and development of the sales helper for Small Business Group (SBG) can be dated back to Sales and Service Negotiation and Quality (SSNQ). Several prototypes and usability studies have been developed and
conducted. Similar stand-alone systems have also been designed,
implemented and deployed in the Home Office Channel of SBG and the
Priority Channel of H&PS. Usability studies have also been conducted for
those systems. For clarity and historical reasons, the Sales Helper for SBG
will be used interchangeably with Target Information and Profile System (TIPS), and the Consultative Sales Profile Environment (CSPE) will be used
in the same way as the Sales Helper for H&PS.
The purpose of the CCOE Sales Helper is to collect customer site profile
items for mass market customers and then to generate solution
recommendations based on the needs and profile of the customer at that
site. The interface provides a user layer for SSCs to initiate, conduct, and
facilitate consultative sales activities. Although the CCOE Sales Helper application focuses on support for Mass Market requirements, other market
units may find that this user interface design extends to their requirements.
This document describes the specifications for the user interface. It does not
address the CCOE Consultative Sales functionality or design. The Customer
Contact Operating Environment Consultative Säles Functional Specification
provides detailed information on the functional specifications for the CCОЕ
Consultative Sales application. This particular version is an interim draft
release of the Sales Helper. The design of CCOE Sales Helper will have to
be modified to accommodate FMO (Future Mode of Operations)requirements.

## 懸置 / epoché

The Sales Helper (TIPS/CSPE) system is part of the overall CCOE system
identified through the re-engineering effort and is sponsored by both SBG
and H&PS of US WEST Communications Inc. It forms one of the basic
components in attaining total customer satisfaction by capturing, distributing, and utilizing expert consultative sales knowledge through the development of a knowledge-based system.



 ---

## 理解 / wissen

The overall objective of the Sales Helper is to organize, represent and
present sales, product, and marketing knowledge in a form that can be
shared, collected,communicated, and utilized effectively by SSCs and other
departments involved.
In the initial design and implementation, the system will be an intelligent
sales tool that is fully and seamlessly integrated with the Customer Contact
Operating Environment (CCOE). It will generate customized product
recommendations based on the telecommunication needs of the customers.
Because of the possible changes of scope of CCOE, the Sales Helper may
have to be redesigned to take into account the FMO requirements identified
in the business re-engineering process. This interim release of the user
interface specification serves both as a closure for the requirements of the
transition from the Present Mode of Operation (PMO+) to the FMO and as
a spring board to the FMO.
The Sales Helper user interface will be developed using X-Windows technology which conforms to OSF/Motif standards. In addition to the
keyboard, SSCs willuse a mouse as a pointing device to control the display of information on their windows.
