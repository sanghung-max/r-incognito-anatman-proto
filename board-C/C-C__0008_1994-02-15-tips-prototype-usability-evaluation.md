## 觀景 / vista

This document discusses the results of a usability evaluation to obtain feedback from
Small Business Group (SBG) sales representatives on Target Information and Profile
System Prototype in Phoenix, Minneapolis and Seattle. Questionnaires and
individual interviews assessed the usability and likability of TIPS panels and the
profile items and their categories. Comments were also solicited on design and
navigational issues.


---

## 亂語 / text

The goal of this usability evaluation was to obtain feedback-from Small Business Group (SBG) sales
representatives on the Target Information and Profile System (TIPS) Prototype. TIPS is the sales
assistant module of SSNQ and uses a graphical user interface designed to support consultative sales
activities of the sales representatives (sales reps) in the business service centers. The sales reps can
collect, view and update customer site profile information through the profile panels in the TIPS
Manager window. The underlying knowledge-based engine will provide a set of possible product
recommendations appropriate to the needs of the customers from their profile information. This
evaluation focused on the usability of the profile items and categories as well as the profile items
panels, the summary panel (under TIPS Manager window) and the recommendation window.

---

## 懸置 / epoché

Eighteen SBG sales representatives from the Phoenix, Minneapolis and Seattle Business Offices
responded to written usability questionnaires and participated in individual interviews. The
questionnaires evaluated the overall usability of TIPS and the usability, likability, and other general
usability issues of each individual window. Comments were solicited on individual panels, specific
profile items and categories, window design and navigational issues and the overall interface during
the interviews.


 ---

## 理解 / wissen

Overall, the results from the usability testing were positive. The sales representatives (sales reps) rated
the majority of the windows and profile items as easy to use. Comments on the profile items windows
revealed that some of the sales reps question the amount of information that they gather and the
resulting impact on customer contact time.
The ratings from the general usability questionnaire were generally favorable. The sales reps feel that
the profile panels will prompt them to ask more consultative questions and the summary panel will help
them to establish rapport quickly with the customers in subsequent contacts. Most of them like the
product benefits offered by the recommendation panel. When they were asked whether they will use
TIPS to support their consultative sales contact with their customers (in questionnaires), 14 out of 18
(78%) reps responded that they would. One had no opinion (5%) and the other three (17%) had
negative responses. Fourteen of the respondents (78%) thought that TIPS would add more value to
consultative sales. Two had (11%) no opinion and the other two (11%) had negative responses.
In the interviews, some of the sales reps expressed concerns on the adjustments of work style and the
time required to complete TIPS during the customer contact.
