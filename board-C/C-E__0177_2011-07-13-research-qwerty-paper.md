## Chinese

### Perplexity | 2026 年 8 月 | 研究論文摘要

〈Mobile QWERTY User Research〉（Hung、Yang、Vance、Kang，2011）說明 Samsung 團隊如何以系統性人因與可用性研究，建立行動 QWERTY 鍵盤的設計建議與效能／滿意度模型。研究涵蓋實體與虛擬鍵盤，核心目標是分離並檢驗不同設計變項對文字輸入速度、準確度及使用者主觀評價的影響，進而支援新裝置與鍵盤原型的開發。

方法上，團隊結合低擬真紙本或泡棉模型、受控的虛擬鍵盤建構工具，以及市售與預發布裝置的競品基準測試。可能時採受試內、平衡排序設計，並遮蔽裝置或原型的品牌資訊，以減少順序與品牌偏好造成的影響。受試者在一分鐘內、不得修正錯誤的條件下輸入預設字串；速度以每分鐘字元數（CPM）衡量，準確度以輸入字串與範本間的平均 Levenshtein 距離衡量，另以綜合績效指標整合速度與錯誤。主觀部分則以 Likert 量表蒐集文字、數字與符號輸入的易用性、鍵帽標示清晰度及整體滿意度。

實體鍵盤研究測量了按鍵高寬、鍵程、觸發力、鍵盤尺寸、按鍵中心距、鍵列數與空白鍵寬度等特徵。一次比較 LG Ally、HTC G2 與 Samsung Epic 的研究中，54 位受試者使用混合字母、數字與符號輸入；LG Ally 與 Samsung Epic 在排名、整體滿意度、速度與準確度上均優於 HTC G2，Epic 的按鍵標示清晰度也較佳。另一項涵蓋 12 款實體 QWERTY 手機、119 名受試者的 PLS 迴歸分析指出，空白鍵寬度是影響效能及滿意度最重要的變項；其次包括鍵程、鍵間縫隙高度與寬度、垂直中心距及鍵列數。研究同時指出，速度、易用性與滿意度未必與準確度完全同向變化，顯示鍵盤設計存在效能取捨。

在佈局研究方面，作者利用相同外形的觸控裝置及可重建的虛擬鍵盤，隔離按鍵機械結構的影響，檢驗尺寸、列欄數、數字與符號配置，以及交錯式與網格式排列。結果顯示，使用者偏好獨立數字列；五列式行動 QWERTY 較具「容錯性」；網格式排列的表現也優於交錯式排列。虛擬鍵盤比較則發現，較大的直向觸控螢幕通常提高滿意度；由 4.0 吋增加至 4.3 吋可降低典型文字輸入的錯誤，但此效果不一定延伸至混合輸入任務。文中報告 Samsung 佈局在多項滿意度、易用性與錯誤率指標上通常優於所重建的 iPhone 4／iPad 佈局，但字詞輸入速度並非在所有比較中占優。最後，作者主張應持續追蹤新裝置，並研究預測式與適應式輸入方法如何疊加於 QWERTY 鍵盤。

## English

### Perplexity | August 2026 | Research-Paper Summary

“Mobile QWERTY User Research” (Hung, Yang, Vance, and Kang, 2011) describes Samsung’s systematic human-factors and usability program for developing design guidance and performance/satisfaction models for mobile QWERTY keyboards. Covering both physical and virtual keyboards, the work aims to isolate the effects of design variables on typing speed, accuracy, and subjective user response, thereby informing new-device and keyboard-prototype development.

The research combines low-fidelity paper and foam mockups, configurable virtual-keyboard tools, and competitive benchmarking of market and pre-launch devices. Where feasible, it uses within-subject, counterbalanced designs and conceals device branding to reduce order and brand-preference effects. Participants type predefined strings for one minute without correcting errors. Speed is measured as characters per minute (CPM), accuracy through mean Levenshtein distance between typed and target strings, and a composite index incorporates speed and error. Subjective ratings use Likert scales for text, number, and symbol entry, key-label clarity, and overall typing satisfaction.

For physical keyboards, the studies measure key height and width, key travel, trip force, keyboard dimensions, center-to-center key spacing, number of rows, and spacebar width. In a comparison involving the LG Ally, HTC G2, and Samsung Epic, 54 participants entered mixed letters, numbers, and symbols. The LG Ally and Samsung Epic exceeded the HTC G2 in overall ranking, satisfaction, speed, and accuracy; Epic’s key markings also received clearer ratings. A separate PLS regression study of 12 physical-QWERTY phones with 119 participants identified spacebar width as the most important physical variable associated with performance and satisfaction. Other influential variables included travel, gap height and width, vertical key-center distance, and row count. The analysis also indicates a design trade-off: factors associated with speed, ease, and satisfaction do not necessarily move accuracy in the same direction.

For layout research, the authors use controlled virtual keyboards on equivalent touch devices to separate layout effects from mechanical-key effects. They examine keyboard size, row and column counts, placement of symbols and numbers, and staggered versus grid configurations. Users preferred a separate number row, five-row mobile QWERTY designs were more “fault-tolerant,” and grid layouts produced better performance than staggered layouts. In virtual-keyboard comparisons, larger portrait displays generally increased satisfaction; moving from 4.0 to 4.3 inches reduced errors in ordinary text-entry tasks, though not necessarily in mixed-entry tasks. Samsung layouts generally showed better satisfaction, ease-of-use, ranking, and error outcomes than reconstructed iPhone 4/iPad layouts, although they did not lead in every word-typing speed comparison. The authors conclude by calling for continued benchmarking of new devices and for research on predictive and adaptive input methods layered onto QWERTY keyboards.

##  拆建/ deconstruct

### Perplexity | 2026 年 8 月 | Deconstruct

- **「最佳」的延異：**文本把「最佳實務」建構為空白鍵寬度、鍵程、鍵距與列數等可排序變項的結果；但「最佳」只在特定模板、單分鐘輸入、特定裝置與量表之內成立，不能脫離其測試裝置而自足。
- **客觀性悖論：**隱藏品牌、平衡順序與虛擬佈局旨在清除干擾，卻以預設英語式 QWERTY、CPM、字串距離及滿意度量表重新劃定何者可見、可計算、可稱為使用者經驗。
- **平滑／條紋空間：**虛擬鍵盤允許佈局、列數與尺寸的可變組合，構成近似平滑的設計探索空間；測試協定、固定字串、裝置尺寸和性能指標則將其再條紋化為可比較、可管理的產品決策空間。
- **缺席痕跡：**文中未充分呈現語言差異、慣用手、長期適應、預測輸入與自然對話情境；它們不是被證偽的因素，而是模型邊界外仍可能改寫結論的痕跡。

### Claude Sonnet 5 | 2026年8月 | Deconstruct

- 核心悖論：文本宣稱「隱藏品牌以避免偏見」("branding information...was hidden to avoid potential bias")，卻在標題與結論中反覆彰顯Samsung優於Apple/HTC/LG——去品牌化的方法論姿態與品牌競爭的敘事目的形成內在張力，中立科學話語實為競品行銷證據之生產機制。

- Component t2的速度/準確度反向相關本身即為解構點：論文的線性因果敘事(more spacebar width→better performance)在此出現裂縫，顯示模型內部存在未被言明的補償結構(trade-off logic)。
