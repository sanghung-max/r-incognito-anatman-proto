### 微觀世界 MICROCOSM   
**光頭 B**

#### 基本概念 

自然世界中的現象和事物關係都可以從科學實驗 及方法中探求出來,從這些實驗結果,科學家試圖尋 找一些模型來解釋這些關係,這些模型都有可以測量 的物理性質。理論物理學就是建築於這一類模型上, 透過抽象的數學符號,導出一些可以測量的結果,這 些理論都經過不斷的驗証及改良,描繪出自然現象。 現代物理學都是環繞着粒子、作用力及場這幾個 概念發展出來,宇宙中的物質都是由細微的原子所組 成,而這些原子更可以被分解為一些本粒子,至於 這些基本粒子是否能夠被無限地分割,科學家也沒有 一定的結論,我們只知道若要更加精細地分解物質 就要用更高的能量。 自然世界的現象都可以透過物質的相互作用力來 描寫及解釋,日常生活的物體以至星雲星團的離子, 都是由九十二種自然元素所組成,不同元素的原子都 帶着不同數目的質子,宇宙中最豐富的元素也是最 單的元素一**氫**。

#### 原子的結構及基本粒子 

普通的一個原子有一個帶有正電荷的原子核,由 中子、質子等組成,整個原子的質量都集中在原子核 ,它的體積很小,密度却很大,直徑大約只有10-13 厘米,雖然質子帶有正電荷,互相排斥,但有一種强 作用力將中子及質子(統稱强子)緊聚在一起,形成 原子核,帶有負電荷的電子就圍繞著原子核。在原子 內部,每一個電子都是在一個確定的量化的狀態中環 繞原子核運轉,不能連續地改變它的運轉狀態。根據 量子力學,微觀世界的現象及事件都是非連續的、量子化的,所有轉變都是根著普朗克常數(Planck's Constant)的級數而改變。 夸克模型把包括質子和中子的所有强子描寫為由 叫做克的較小實體構成的複合粒子。而物質的基本 粒子是六種輕子(leptons)及六味克(quarks), 每一「味」(flavour) 克有三種「色」(colour) 所謂「味」和「色」只是代表不同的克及其不同的 特性。有些物理學家更加將克分解為更「基本」的 基本粒子。有人叫這些「粒子」為「毛子」,因為毛澤東相信克是可以分割的!

#### 基本粒子與作用力 

自然界的四種作用力,都由一些規范理論來描寫 ,這些力的性質是根據物理學定律中的對稱性或規則 性推出來。在正常的溫度或能量下,一切自然現象都 取決於這四種基本力一引力、電磁力、弱力和强力。 這些力與少數附加參量(如粒子質量)一起決定了宇 宙的結構和特徵。 在量子力學中,粒子本身可以表示為場。兩個粒 子通過它們的互相滲透場發生的相互作用可以看為兩 個粒子交換了第三個粒子,這個粒子稱為場的量子。 現在夸克的相互作用是由一種以相稱於「色」的 定域變換的不變性為礎的規範理論來描寫。為了保 持這種不變性,需要十六個場。它們的場量子是由八 種叫做膠子(gluons)的無質量矢量玻色子(bosons) 構成,每一種都有「色」及「反色」的組合。傳遞電 磁力及弱力的是Weinberg- Salam- Ward模型的規 范粒子,即無質量的光子及甚大矢量玻色子的三重態 W+、W-和Z。引力是由於交換無質量的、自旋為 二的粒子(引力子)而引起的,引力子(gravitons) 是由另一種定域規范理論(廣義相對論)來描寫。

#### 統一場論 

統一場論嘗試將基本粒子及作用力統一,物理學 家利用SU (5) 的理論去描寫力場的統一,在極高的 能量(1015 GeV)或極短的距離(102cm)下,所有的 作用力及基本粒子的狀態都會成為一種的粒子及作用 力,科學家稱該種粒子為X粒子。 在人類的實驗室中也許永遠也不能達到這樣高的 能量,物理學家認為在宇宙誕生後10-4秒時,宇宙中 所有粒子都類似X粒子(當時宇宙的溫度為101K)力 場也是統一,當宇宙的溫度降低時,各類的基本粒子及作用力也隨著出現。

### 大爆論

我們對於整個宇宙的認識,是透過觀測天體所發 出的電磁波(光是一種電磁波)及粒子所得出來的, 電磁波是由電場及磁場波動產生的,它在真空中有絕 對的速度—————每秒3×108米,宇宙中任何物體也不能 超過光速(電磁波的速度),這些電磁波正好反映出 天體內部物質的結構及演化過程,宇宙中有很多不同 類型的天體,有著不同的特性及歷史,發出不同的粒 子及電磁波。 研究天體的結構及演化過程可以利用天體的光譜, 光譜是將天體的電磁波分析為不同波長的電磁波。從 光譜中的光譜線可以測定天體內部的原子或離子狀態 ,也可以決定組成該天體的元素、該天體的溫度及速 度。 大部份的物質都是由氫(Hydrogen)及氣(Helium) 所組成,其它的元素只佔很少份量,較重的元 素是由於超新星(一些將近滅亡的恆星)爆炸中的高 熱產生出來。

我們的太陽是一顆踏入中年的恆星,恆星是透過 熱核反應而發出電磁波及粒子的天體,太陽只是千千 固恆星之一,這些恆星及其它天體(如星雲星團等) 組成一個系統,稱為星系,星系的形狀倒像一隻煎蛋 ,星系的中心由一些較老的恆星(紅巨星)及一些球 狀星團所組成,在這個中心週圍有數個旋臂,由一些 較年青的恆星及星際物質合成,一個星系的直徑大約 是十萬光年,厚度也有數千至萬餘光年。天文學家發 現宇宙是由很多星系所組成。 最近我們星系的星系是仙女座大星系,距離我們 二百萬光年,換句話說,它的光芒是二百萬年前的影 像,在我們附近的星系組成所謂星系團,這些星系都 受到彼此的引力影響,天文學家相信宇宙的直徑是一 百億光年左右。 在宇宙的邊緣有一些所謂類星體的存在( quasars),天文學家相信這些類星體是宇宙大爆炸後所留下的證據。

目前最流行的宇宙論是大爆論( Big-bang Theory),從星系光譜的紅移現象可以推到宇宙是在膨 脹,以目前的膨脹着的宇宙向後推到星系形成之前, 宇宙學家把宇宙起源追溯到一個奇點,這一個奇點表 示大約一百億年前的空間及時間的開始,在那個時候 之前,今天已知的物理定律都不適用。 奇點的特徵很可能是無限大的密度和曲率,宇宙 學奇點類似於黑洞事界中的奇點,在這個奇點之內的 物質及光綫受到引力的吸持。沒有任何東西能離開這 些奇點,因為要離開引力控制所需的速度大於光速, 而相對論又要求光速不可能超過。 迄今還不知道宇宙膨脹會否永遠繼續下去,或者 是否會在某時星系彼此停止退行,朝相反方向運動, 最後聚集在一起。無限膨脹意味著「開放」宇宙, 繼 之以塌縮的有限膨脹表示「封閉」宇宙。

### **Basic Concepts**

The phenomena and relational structures of the natural world can be investigated through scientific experiments and methods. From these experimental results, scientists attempt to construct models that explain these relationships; these models possess measurable physical properties. Theoretical physics is built upon such models, using abstract mathematical symbols to derive measurable outcomes. These theories undergo constant testing and refinement, allowing us to depict natural phenomena.

Modern physics develops around the concepts of **particles**, **forces**, and **fields**. All matter in the universe is composed of tiny atoms, and atoms themselves can be decomposed into more fundamental particles. Whether these fundamental particles can be divided without limit remains inconclusive; what we do know is that probing matter at finer scales requires higher energies.

Natural phenomena can be described and explained through the interactions among matter. Everyday objects and even ions in nebulae and star clusters are composed of the ninety‑two naturally occurring elements. Atoms of different elements carry different numbers of protons. The most abundant element in the universe is also the simplest: **hydrogen**.

### **Atomic Structure and Fundamental Particles**

A typical atom contains a positively charged nucleus composed of neutrons and protons. Nearly all the atom’s mass is concentrated in this nucleus, which is extremely small and extremely dense, with a diameter of roughly 10−13 cm. Although protons repel each other due to their positive charge, a **strong force** binds neutrons and protons (collectively called **hadrons**) tightly together to form the nucleus. Negatively charged electrons orbit around the nucleus.

Inside the atom, each electron occupies a definite, quantized state; it cannot change its orbital state continuously. According to quantum mechanics, phenomena and events in the microscopic world are discontinuous and quantized — all transitions occur in discrete steps determined by the **Planck constant**.

The **quark model** describes all hadrons (including protons and neutrons) as composite particles made of smaller entities called **quarks**. The fundamental particles of matter consist of six **leptons** and six **flavors** of quarks. Each quark flavor comes in three **colors**. “Flavor” and “color” simply denote different types of quarks and their properties. Some physicists have attempted to decompose quarks into even more “fundamental” particles. Some jokingly call these hypothetical entities **“Mao‑particles”**, because Mao Zedong believed quarks could be further divided!

### **Fundamental Particles and Forces**

The four fundamental forces of nature are described by **gauge theories**. Their properties are derived from symmetries and regularities in physical laws. At ordinary temperatures and energies, all natural phenomena are governed by these four basic forces: **gravity**, **electromagnetism**, the **weak force**, and the **strong force**. Together with a few additional parameters (such as particle masses), they determine the structure and characteristics of the universe.

In quantum mechanics, a particle can be represented as a field. The interaction between two particles can be viewed as the exchange of a third particle — the **quantum of the field**.

Quark interactions are described by a gauge theory based on invariance under local transformations corresponding to “color.” Maintaining this invariance requires sixteen fields. Their quanta are eight massless vector bosons called **gluons**, each carrying combinations of color and anticolor.

Electromagnetic and weak forces are transmitted by the gauge particles of the **Weinberg–Salam–Ward model**: the massless **photon** and the massive vector bosons **W⁺**, **W⁻**, and **Z**.

Gravity arises from the exchange of massless spin‑2 particles called **gravitons**, described by another gauge theory — **general relativity**.

### **Unified Field Theory**

Unified field theory attempts to unify fundamental particles and forces. Physicists use the **SU(5)** model to describe the unification of force fields. At extremely high energies (1015 GeV) or extremely short distances (10−32 cm), all forces and particle states converge into a single type of particle and a single type of force. Scientists call this particle the **X‑particle**.

Human laboratories may never reach such energies. Physicists believe that around 10−4 seconds after the birth of the universe — when the temperature was 1015 K — all particles resembled X‑particles and all forces were unified. As the universe cooled, different fundamental particles and forces emerged.

### **The Big Bang Theory**

Our understanding of the universe comes from observing electromagnetic waves (light is one form) and particles emitted by celestial bodies. Electromagnetic waves arise from oscillations of electric and magnetic fields and travel through vacuum at an absolute speed — 3×108 meters per second. Nothing in the universe can exceed the speed of light. These waves reflect the internal structure and evolutionary processes of celestial bodies.

Astronomers analyze celestial structures and evolution using **spectra** — decompositions of electromagnetic waves into different wavelengths. Spectral lines reveal the atomic or ionic states inside celestial bodies, the elements composing them, their temperatures, and their velocities.

Most matter consists of **hydrogen** and **helium**; heavier elements are produced in the intense heat of **supernova explosions**.

Our Sun is a middle‑aged star. Stars emit electromagnetic waves and particles through **thermonuclear reactions**. The Sun is just one of countless stars. Stars and other celestial bodies (nebulae, clusters, etc.) form systems called **galaxies**. A galaxy resembles a fried egg: older stars (red giants) and globular clusters form the central bulge, while younger stars and interstellar matter form spiral arms around it. A galaxy’s diameter is about 100,000 light‑years, with a thickness of several thousand to over ten thousand light‑years. Astronomers have discovered that the universe is composed of many galaxies.

The nearest major galaxy to ours is the **Andromeda Galaxy**, about two million light‑years away — meaning its light shows us how it looked two million years ago. Nearby galaxies form **clusters**, bound by mutual gravitational attraction. Astronomers estimate the universe’s diameter to be around ten billion light‑years.

At the edge of the universe lie objects called **quasars**, believed to be remnants or evidence of the Big Bang.

The most widely accepted cosmological model today is the **Big Bang Theory**. From the redshift of galaxy spectra, we infer that the universe is expanding. By extrapolating backward from the current expansion, cosmologists trace the universe’s origin to a **singularity** — the beginning of space and time roughly ten billion years ago. Before that moment, known physical laws do not apply.

The singularity likely possessed infinite density and curvature, similar to the singularity inside a black hole’s event horizon. Matter and light within such a singularity are trapped by gravity; nothing can escape because doing so would require speeds exceeding the speed of light, which relativity forbids.

It remains unknown whether the universe will expand forever or eventually halt and reverse, causing galaxies to move toward one another and collapse. Eternal expansion corresponds to an **open universe**; eventual collapse corresponds to a **closed universe**.
