#### 原文:  O 狼   1983

還記得中學時代已時常「較腳」去看「毛」片，初時看這些片子都會帶著戰兢兢的心情，撞著親戚朋友或老師？大家都不好意思。初期的色情電影都較soft-cored，什麼Emmanuelle，Bilitis等都拍攝得頗美，可說得上是一種感官享受，隨著電影檢查制度的放鬆，影片也越來越大胆、無論男性、女性生殖器的鏡頭，都在銀幕上出現了。也看過一些X-RATED（甚至XXX-RATED）電影、裏面有各種性行為的大特寫，男與女、女與女、男與男、人與獸、三男一女……等等。

越看色情電影，就越覺得自己是督伯，簡直是貼錢買難受，冇癮要俾錢睇人造愛。若果是拍得vulgar的，使人作嘔。拍得seductive的，更令人渾身不舒服，心思思，有件事，遇疑難，唔話得俾人知，總之是虐待自己。

大部份色情電影都沒有新意，只是靠造愛的花式或技術叫座，由X-RATED到XXX-RATED的影片，都不外如是，最多是標榜一些變態的性行為，拍攝大小二便、coprophilia（由糞便得到性滿足）、Necrophilia（姦屍——林過雲也拍過，更加真實）、bestiality（和狗、羊或馬等性交）、Saliromania（在女人的身體或衣服排洩大小便而得到性滿足）……

若果要read这些色情電影時，我們可以從那些角度來看呢？


在色情電影中的女人，大多被視爲一種商品或男性的附屬品，沒有自己的尊嚴及價值。這不但反映出女性在现代社會中的地位，而且還觸及一套深入人心的意識型態。


慾、生、性、死都是人類（及野獸）的生命過程，這些經驗都帶著強烈的情感及官感的刺激，暴力、性慾及侵略慾都是動物世界中的「森林定律」，所謂「人性」也有這些「獸性」。當人要進入

社會生活時，就需要壓抑一定的「獸性」，所謂潛意識，就是這個壓制下的產品。在個人的心理發展階段中，自我形象的形成是對以後生活方式及信念的關鍵，每一個社會都有著一些規範及律例，管制社會成員的行為及想法，較普遍的規範都會從各種不同的社會制度灌輸給其成員，家庭、學校甚至傳播媒介都成為傳遞這些生活規範及意識型態的工具。而家長、老師、「前鬣」也成為這些「教育」工作的執行官。

至於「生、老、病、死、性慾」等問題大都會被神秘化，宗教是虛理和調節這些極度情感化的現象的社會制度，對這些問題的禁忌、神話及儀式也隨著衍生，有限度地解決這些問題。正因為這些問題會帶來情緒的不穩定及恐懼，大部份社會成員都會接受這些解決方法為最終極的、或最完美的，毫不猶疑地接受這些解決方式。

但這套「道德／宇宙／世界」觀在面對現代生活的挑戰時，卻根本站不住腳。科技的興起、城市的生活方式等，都使一些傳統的價值觀崩潰，人也要生活在「無意義」或「無規範」的情境下，而那些極度情感化的現象隨即湧現。存在於社會中的空虛感、無能感及失落感，往往驅使著人從各種極端的經驗中尋找自我。性行為、死亡、瘋狂及各種潮流也成爲人類追尋自己的遊戲。

  
色情電影是對傳統價值觀崩潰的一個回應。將女人、暴力及性慾客體化，須成一堆堆可以交換及買賣的商品，消費者可以用金錢及時間買來一些官感的滿足。色情電影更將女人、性慾等轉化成一些旣神秘，又可怕，卻又具吸引力的符號，直接向觀衆的潛意識開刀。


從這些分析，可以引申到現代資本主義社會的運作及邏輯，或資本家經濟及政治的利益等，但將現代色情電影單一地約化成主導意識型態的產品，就是忽略了很多人的本能及性質。

傳統的社會或極權的政治制度都不能容忍所有極端的現象或東西（例如色情電影），因為這些現象正好代表了反叛，危險及混亂。但在一個較開放的社會中，極端現象也可能被人利用來達到經濟和政治之目的。

  
回到個體的心理及感情，兩性關係是人類最親密及原始的關係，這個關係的「意義」是建築於主體／主體的「終極接觸」上一It's the consummation of a heavenly feast-love。很多色情電影將這個主體／主體關係割裂，成為主體／客體的關係，性行為也被貶為機械式的，無生命活力的行為——種沒有靈性交通的禽獸行為，性伴侶也變成洩慾的工具。

  
性愛是一個 living and lived 的社會關係，色情電影所描繪的只是意象的關係，沒有實在的內涵，觀衆很容易生活在這些幻象之中，接受、加強及加深對影片中意識型態的認同。

  
色情電影還有一種將人（包括男人、女人、兒童）貶低為物體的趨勢，普通的性愛鏡頭已經不能滿足觀衆的口味，色情電影的製片人將更令人產生極端情緒反應的材料搬上銀幕（例如極度S-M的電影及小孩/成人之性愛片等。這個趨向也反映出人的形像正在瓦解，現代的科技及知識打破了很多與人形像關的神話，毀滅了這些神話淩，卻一無所有！只餘下一些獸性？

  
或許人所感到的空虚、失落及憂慮的感覺可以從性行爲中暫時填補，官能的刺激、個人的投入，使人忘記生活的重擔。看龍一齣色情電影就好像發了一場綺夢一般，整個人有點迷惘，鏡頭又誘發了很多性幻想，片子使觀衆部份認同男女主角（或配角），在幻想中創造一個異性的形象來達到滿足，但這個異性形象只是破碎的、不完全的——只是自我中所凝聚的異性特點之投射，換句話說，在這個認同/幻想過程中，人的心理狀態是雙性的——是hermaphroditic。

大部份人都會將內在化之異性特點否定及壓抑，傳統的性別分工及社會規範大大限制了個體性格的發展，而壓制的結果是將潛意識和日常生活分割，但潛意識卻無時無刻地影響著信念及態度，若果一個人不能認識或將潛意識轉化爲意識的話，就很容易釀出悲劇，觀看色情電影是一個認識自己的潛意識的方法。

最後請大家不要變成 peeping Tom，在戲院中可以幻想自己是偷窺狂，在其他地方就不好了，下次看完毛片時，若果到喉唔到肺，記得飲囉幾杯涼茶。

### The Text Reconstructed

I remember back in my secondary school days, we’d often "sneak off" to catch a "hair-piece" (porn flick). Back then, it was all heart-thumping nerves—what if we bumped into an uncle, a friend, or god forbid, a teacher? The awkwardness was real. Early porn was relatively "soft-core"—flicks like Emmanuelle or Bilitis were shot quite beautifully, a genuine sensory treat. But as censorship loosened, the films got bolder. Suddenly, full-frontal shots of every variety were splashed across the silver screen. I’ve sat through X-rated (even XXX) features with extreme close-ups of every act imaginable: male/female, female/female, male/male, man/beast, three-men-one-lady… you name it.

The more I watched, the more I felt like a "Do-Bak" (a lecherous old fool). It was literally paying money to suffer—spending hard-earned cash to watch other people "do it" without getting any satisfaction yourself. If it was filmed vulgarly, it was nauseating. If it was filmed seductively, it left you feeling restless and itchy, carrying a secret burden you couldn't tell a soul. Pure self-torture.

Most porn lacks any shred of originality, relying solely on "positions" or "techniques" to sell tickets. From X to XXX, it’s all the same old story, at most hyping up some perversions: filming excretory acts, coprophilia (sexual pleasure from feces), necrophilia (corpse-fucking—even the "Rainy Night Butcher" Lam Kor-wan filmed his, for that "authentic" touch), bestiality (sex with dogs, sheep, or horses), saliromania (pleasure from defiling a woman’s body or clothes)…

If we are to "read" these pornographic films, from what angles should we look?

In porn, women are mostly treated as mere commodities or male appendages, stripped of dignity or intrinsic value. This doesn’t just reflect the status of women in modern society; it touches upon a deeply rooted ideology.

Lust, life, sex, and death are the life processes of humans (and beasts alike). These experiences carry intense emotional and sensory stimulation. Violence, libido, and aggression are the "laws of the jungle" in the animal kingdom, and so-called "humanity" harbours these "bestial" traits. Upon entering social life, one must suppress a certain amount of this "beast." The "subconscious" is precisely the product of this suppression.

However, this "moral/cosmic" worldview crumbles when faced with modern life. The rise of technology and urban lifestyles has collapsed traditional values. People live in a state of "meaninglessness" or "anomie," where those extreme emotional phenomena resurface. The void, the impotence, and the sense of loss in society drive people to seek themselves in extreme experiences. Sex, death, madness—these become the games humans play to find their own reflection.

Porn is a response to the collapse of traditional values. It objectifies women, violence, and lust, turning them into piles of tradable commodities. It targets the viewer's subconscious with symbols that are mysterious, terrifying, yet magnetic.

Returning to the individual psyche: the bond between genders is the most intimate and primal human relationship. Its "meaning" is built upon a subject-to-subject "ultimate contact"—It's the consummation of a heavenly feast-love. Most porn severs this subject-to-subject link, turning it into a subject-to-object relationship. Sex is degraded into a mechanical, lifeless act—a beastly act devoid of spiritual communion. The partner becomes a mere tool for release.

Sex is a living and lived social relationship. Porn depicts only "image-based" relationships with no actual substance. Viewers easily inhabit these illusions, reinforcing their identification with the film's ideology.

There is a trend in porn to degrade humans (men, women, children) into "objects." Ordinary sex scenes no longer satisfy; producers bring more extreme material to the screen (like hardcore S&M or adult/child films). This trend reflects the disintegration of the human image. Modern tech and knowledge have shattered the myths surrounding the "human." After the myths are destroyed, we are left with nothing but... bestiality?

Perhaps the void and anxiety can be temporarily filled by sex. The sensory jolt and personal immersion make one forget the burdens of life. Watching a porn flick is like a fever dream—you leave the theater dazed, the shots triggering a cascade of sexual fantasies. But the image of the "other" created in these fantasies is broken and incomplete—merely a projection of one's own internalized opposite traits. In other words, in this identification process, the psychological state is hermaphroditic.

Most people suppress these internalized traits of the opposite sex. Traditional gender roles and social norms severely limit personality development. This suppression splits the subconscious from daily life. If one cannot recognize or transform the subconscious into consciousness, tragedy ensues. Watching porn is, in a way, a method to encounter your own subconscious.

Finally, please don't turn into a "Peeping Tom." It's fine to fantasize about being a voyeur in the cinema, but don't take it elsewhere. Next time you finish a "hair-piece," if it feels "stuck in your throat" (unsatisfied), remember to go grab a few cups of herbal tea to cool your jets.
