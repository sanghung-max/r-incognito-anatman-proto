### 後記

#### (特稿:《學苑》八三至八四年度第一期 科學與科技)

科學是一種語言遊戲。它代表着一套經整理、有系統的、關於這個世界的經驗知識體系。科學的實踐,是由一套所謂「科學方法」所界定的標準程序與慣例所規範。這種方法,本質上界定了一個歸納式的、螺旋式上升的過程——資料蒐集、假設形成、理論與模型建構,以至預測之產生。科學的實踐者可以就各種處境設計出試探性的解決方案,加以實驗,並根據觀察所得予以修訂或摒棄。任何一門科學學科的追求,都是一種由科學家社群所發起及維繫的社會活動。一般而言,任何科學學科的論述領域,均受該學科參與者所共同認可的一套或多套「範式」所界定,而這些範式又蘊含着一系列信念、規則與實踐。這些範式受制於該時代的生產方式及意識形態上層建築,反映着該時期的世界觀(Weltanschauung)。任何對主導範式所定規則的違背,均可能招致排斥或迫害。簡而言之,這些範式可視為一種社會與意識形態上的控制形式,約束着參與者的活動。

任何科學學科的主導範式,構成該學科之核心。它為該學科大多數實踐者所篤信,並頗為堅執。有時,它甚至被奉為一種絕對、不可侵犯的教條。然而,一門科學學科的邊緣地帶,卻存在着某些與主導範式相抗衡的範式或詮釋。學科核心與邊緣之間的界線,大抵是模糊而搖擺不定的,並受該時代之社會政治狀況及科技發展水平所左右。

托勒密的地心說範式,在中世紀迫使天文學家以複雜而怪誕的本輪(epi-circles)來解釋行星的運行。此一模型,在某程度上是受當時的主導世界觀所啟發——即人乃全能上帝所造之物,而人居於宇宙之中心。哥白尼提出另一劃時代的範式——太陽系的日心說(地球與行星均繞太陽運行),從而打破了這個模型。此模型對西方世界科學之發展影響深遠,為牛頓力學及各科學領域的勝利鋪平了道路。

本世紀初現代物理學之興起,同樣是對十九世紀物理學主導範式的一次突破。隨着觀測與實驗技術之精進,科學家發現牛頓力學與古典電動力學已無法充分解釋種種物理現象。當時盛行的世界觀——康德式的絕對時空概念,受到相對論之挑戰;能量之連續性概念,則為量子力學中離散量子(quanta)之概念所取代。

一門科學學科由一個主導範式過渡至另一範式,其過程往往充斥着混亂、抗拒與衝突。舊有的慣例與規則可能因而被摧毀。部分實踐者,無論新範式如何嚴謹有力,仍傾向捍衛並固守其舊有迷思,甚至到了自我欺瞞的地步。這些實踐者可能與掌權者結盟,對新模型的信奉者加以迫害。

在我們這個現代世界裏,科學研究往往被建制化,由政府或大型企業所操控,並服膺於一國之內以至各國之間的經濟、政治及軍事場域。科學的追求,在某種意義上,已淪為一場權力的鬥爭——爭奪控制與操縱之權。掌權者界定了科學探究的範疇,並左右着科學知識傳播與流布之過程。科學若運用失當,便會使之淪為核武器與生化控制這場危險遊戲的一部分。

範式的躍遷,往往伴隨着該時代主導世界觀的一場革命性轉變。進化論者假設,包括人類在內的生物,均由無生命物質演化而來。人乃上帝造物之形象,驟然變為猿猴後裔之形象。物理學家亦不得不放棄一些曾被奉若圭臬、幾乎從未受到質疑的概念,當中包括:時間對一切觀察者而言均恆常流逝之概念、事件同時性之概念,以及粒子運動之精確可測性等。

科學知識本質上是假設性的。由科學所導出的世界觀或人的形象,絕非絕對或不可侵犯,而是隨主導範式之更迭而修訂或取代。而這些主導範式,反過來又塑造並影響着該特定時期的世界觀。因此,人類的科學經驗,是動態的、歷史性的,而非靜態的、超歷史的。以此觀之,科學可以成為一股強大的力量,將理論與實踐相連結,把抽象思維轉化為具體存在。

### Postscript
##### (Special: Science and Technology in "Undergrad" 83-84 Issue 1)

Science is a kind of language game. It represents system of organized and empirical knowledge of the world. Its practice is defined by a set of standard procedures and conventions under an assumption known as the scientific method. This method, in essence, defines an inductive, spiral process of data collection, hypothesis formation, theory and model construction and prediction generation. The practitioners of science can devise tentative solutions to various situations, experiment with them and modify or discard them in the light of the observation conducted. The pursuit of any discipline of science is a social activity initiated and sustained by a community of scientists. In general, the definition of the discursive domain of any scientific discipline is bound by paradigm(s) embodying sets of beliefs, rules and practices agreed upon by its participants. These paradigms are regulated by the mode of production and the ideological superstructure peculiar to that time. These paradigms reflect the Weltanschauung or world view of that period. Any breach of the rules defined by the dominant paradigms may result in ostracization or persecution. In a nutshell, these paradigms may serve as a form of social and ideological control restraining the activities of the participants.


The major paradigm of any scientific discipline forms the core of that discipline. It is held by the majority of its practitioners with much tenacity. Sometimes. it may be hailed as a kind of absolute. inviolable dogma. The periphery of a scientific discipline, however, contains certain antagonistic paradigms or interpretations. The boundary between the core and the periphery of a discipline is more or less amorphous and flickering. This boundary is influenced by the socio-political conditions and the level of technological development of that time. 


The geocentric paradigm of Ptolemy compelled astronomers to explain the motion of the planets in terms of complex and weird epi-circles in the Middle Ages. This model was in a way inspired by the dominant world view that man was a creature of an omnipotent God and that man was at the centre of the Universe. Copernicus shattered this model by propounding another revolutionary paradigm- the heliocentric model of the solar system (the Earth and the planets revolve around the Sun). This model had a tremendous impact on the development of science in the Western world, paving the way to the triumph of Newtonian Mechanics and various scientific fields. 


The rise of Modern Physics in the beginning of this century was also a breakthrough in the dominant paradigm of physics in the 19th Century. With refined techniques of observation and experimentation, scientists found that Newtonian Mechanics and classical electrodynamics could not account for physical phenomena adequately. The prevailing world views then-the Kantian concept of absolute space and time was challenged by relativity; the continuous concept of energy was replaced by the concept of discrete quanta in quantum mechanics. 


The transition from a dominant paradigm to another a scientific discipline is usually characterized by confusion, resistance and conflict. Previous conventions and rules may be destroyed. Some of the practitioners tend to defend and preserve their myths up to the point of self-delusion, regardless of the rigor and power of the new paradigm. These practitioners may ally with the political power-holders and persecute the adherents of the new model.


In our modern world, scientific research is often institutionalized and run by the government or large business corporations. It is geared to the economic, political and military arena within a nation and between various nations. The pursuit of science, in some way, has become the struggle for power, for power to control and manipulate. The power- holders define the domain of scientific inquiry and affect the process of the transmission and dissemination of scientific knowledge. The injudicious application of science makes it a dangerous game of nuclear weaponry and biochemical control control. 


The quantum jump of paradigms may be coupled with a revolutionary change in the world view prevalent of that age. Evolutionists hypothesize that living organisms, including man, were evolved from non-living matter. The image of man as God's creature was changed drastically to that of the progeny of the apes. Physicists have had to abandon some concepts which were so firmly held that they had probably never been questioned before. These included the concept of a time flowing constantly for all observers, the concept of the simultaneity of events, the exact specification of the motion of a particle.....


Scientific knowledge is hypothetical. The world view or the image of man derived from science is by no means absolute or inviolable. It is modified or replaced according to the dominant paradigms. These dominant paradigms, in turn, shape and influence the world view at that particular period. Thus, the scientific experience of man is dynamic and historical, rather than static and ahistorical. In this way, science can become a powerful force linking theory with practice and transforming abstract thinking to concrete existence.

## English
### Perplexity | August 2026 | Wissen

- The text’s core prior is that scientific knowledge is provisional, corrigible, and historically situated. Its methodological model moves through data, hypothesis, model, and prediction, then revision or abandonment through observation—akin to knowledge advancing through refutation.
- Evidence updating is figured as a spiral: pressure on Ptolemaic astronomy and experimental inadequacies in Newtonian mechanics and classical electrodynamics enable reorganization through relativity and quantum theory. Yet the essay supplies neither specific experiments nor quantitative comparisons among rival models.
- Falsifiability functions chiefly as a normative ideal constrained by paradigm communities and power: dominant beliefs may be defended while dissent incurs exclusion. Its theory of knowledge thus joins a mechanism of methodological correction to social conditions that can obstruct correction.

### Claude Sonnet 5 | Aug 2026 | Wissen

- The essay's core epistemic commitment leans Kuhnian rather than Popperian: knowledge progress is framed as "paradigm shift" rather than incremental falsification, and normal science is described as tolerating anomalies rather than immediately rejecting them upon disconfirmation — an implicit critique of strict Popperian falsificationism.
- The line "scientific knowledge is hypothetical... modified or replaced according to the dominant paradigms" borrows Popperian vocabulary ("hypothetical") while the actual mechanism described (paradigms determined by community/ideology/power structure) is closer to Kuhnian social constructivism — the two epistemic frameworks sit unreconciled side by side, producing a methodological hybrid rather than a single coherent stance.
- The essay attributes paradigm shifts to socio-political conditions and modes of production ("regulated by the mode of production and the ideological superstructure"), grafting a Marxist sociology-of-knowledge frame onto the Kuhnian scaffold — at least three distinct epistemic traditions (Kuhn, quasi-Popper, Marxist) are layered without the author reconciling them explicitly.
- The closing pivot to nuclear/biochemical weaponry is not a rigorously argued extension but an evidentially unsupported inferential leap (from "paradigm shift" directly to "dangerous game of manipulation") — an affect-driven rather than logic-driven conclusion, marking an internal rupture in the text's own epistemic rigor.
