## 亂語 / text

### GODOG | September 2026

These essays marked the majority of GODOG's and R-Incognito's religious and spirituality essays.  They included assignment submitted for a mandatory class in the Baptist College and essays dated from 1978.  The Obsequies of the Gods was written in 1978 and published in 1982. The "nickname" GODOG had been used since 1979.  The first essay (Thanatos) was written with this pseudonym in 1982.  GODOG wrote 4 essays, 2 of which were published in <Undergrad 83-84>. 

GODOG did not share the 2 unpublished essays ("Dialog between GODOG as Anti Christ and Christ", "In the Beginning is the Word/Way") with anyone until 2016 or 2017.  

As iterated before, GODOG does not write to publish or try to win any literary accolade.  GODOG writes to document raw lived experience of senses, emotions, thoughts, stream of consciousness.  They serve as written historical records for introspection, examination and analysis - an exercise for reflexive understanding with less chance of fabrication, repression or modifications from memory reconstruction. 

GODOG's writings were marked by using religious/spiritual imageries and language to deconstruct and implode evangelical Christianity from within.  Christianity just happened to be the belief framework GODOG has some familiarity with.  Note that for GODOG (as in other personae), attempts to only critique and deconstruct dogmatic "Absolute" belief systems they have lived experience inside before.  Understanding the psychology, culture and sociology of the believers and their communities from within (and without) are critical to GODOG's  deconstructive efforts.   
 
GODOG grew up in Christian and Catholic educational institutions from kindergarten through college.  From primary school (5+ years) through most of secondary school (4+ years), GODOG attended the same Sunday School run by the Chinese Methodist Church.  He also went to Sunday services after Sunday school with his parents in primary school.  The Sunday school teachers were very progressive at the times.  GODOG was taught Freudian psychoanalysis at the age of 13 (credit went to 姚志陽) who also introduced Reid James' "God, the Atom and the Universe" as reading material.  

The Sunday school and the Sunday Worship Services used the Chinese translation of the King James Bible.  GODOG has 2 life-long friends from the Sunday school (unbeknownst to GODOG and personae pseudonyms and writings).  One is a girl who will still pray for GODOG alter-ego and still talks about God in her life from time to time. The other was a classmate in primary school.  He is a Catholic but he does not talk about his faith at all.  Both are close friends of GODOG and they would disclose their lives without reservation. 

GODOG's secondary school (St. joseph's College) is a Catholic School run by La Salle Brothers.  For better or worse, biblical knowledge was a mandatory subject from Form 1 through Form 5.  All the graduating students were also required to take biblical knowledge examination in the Hong Kong Certificate of Education Examination.  The students were required to recite the 4 Gospels, Acts of Apostles and the Romans of the New Testament from Jerusalem Bible.  GODOG still remembered the stressful time in Form 4 Biblical Knowledge classes.  The brother teaching the course scored students through  random selection of students to stand up and recite verses from the assigned sections of the gospel one after another in each lesson. 

By the time GODOG was in Form 3 or Form 4, he was no longer a believer.   His mind was "poisoned" by amateur astronomers by virtue of his association with Hong Kong Amateur Astronomical Society and by a mentor (another life-long friend a year senior) at St, Joseph's College.  He introduced Bertrand Russell's writings like "A History of Western Civilization" , "Why I am not a Christian" as GODOG's reading.  The irony and legend that GODOG created during that time was the distinction earned from Hong Kong School Certificate Examination in Biblical Knowledge (only distinction that year among all the Josephians, less than 1%).  One could not get top score with recitation alone, the subject required arguments and integration of different books of the gospel.

As of today, GODOG still remembered the comic analogy of Heaven and Hell from a mentor in AAU/AAS (Chan Chu-Luk, nickname "Headmaster") - "Heaven is boring.  You have to sing hymns to praise God every day every moment.  Hell is far more interesting, I will certainly enjoy the frequent burning of hell fire..." 

GODOG did most of the "soul-seeking" activities between Lower/Upper Six and the years at Hong Kong Baptist College - a "conservative" post-secondary college majoring in Physics.  Paradoxically, one of the best religious courses taken by GODOG happened to be from a lecturer who taught "Philosophy of Religion" using part of Wittgenstein's Tractatus as primary teaching material.  A year after though, GODOG also had to take a course taught by a conservative Baptist lady, Mrs. Heckett - culminating in the term end essay "Christ was a paranoid schizophrenic".  By the grace of God, she did not fail GODOG for the effort.  GODOG failed in Quantum Mechanics that semester instead.  Sigh...

As GODOG and personae continued to examine, interpret and deconstruct their early life through abnormal psychology, psychiatry, Christianity and different subjects, they assumed a more sinister pose as in this collection.  

GODOG and personae were also influenced by Teilhard de Chardin's "Phenomenon of Man" at the time.  Traces of Chardin may be found in GODOG and personae even as of today with the understanding that they were at the intersection of metaphysical, theological and scientific realms.  By the time GODOG wrote the first essay "Thanatos" in 1982, most, if not all of the framework and thoughts were relatively well-formed and crystallized with the birth of R. Incognito around 1978.
