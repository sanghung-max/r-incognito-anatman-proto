### GODOG | circa 1984


太初有道，道與世界同在，道就是我。 

道擴散到盡頭，就是黑暗混沌的死巷。無道衍生在馬戲班的鋼線上。枯乾的戰士骸骨在 刮起風沙的大漠中倒下，只是留下一對腐壞的 眼珠。鋼線無限的盡頭就是你。無窮的道跌進 無你、無理、毛履的黑洞，消失在虛無浩瀚的 無道中。

宇宙充滿了孩童的歡呼和唱采，諸神披上 漆黑的斗篷，面貌漸漸溶化，蛆蟲由棕黑的皮 肉、鼻孔鑽出來，熾熱的陰風鞭撻著永不腐朽 的靈魂，小丑的祭禮如常莊嚴地進行……

普世歡騰，道降臨大地。我被逐出穹蒼的 母體，黯然地來到塵世。我愛世人，降生在我 母親的子宮中，應當敬畏, 崇拜你的神。蠟像開始 溶化，神剃光了頭，續一將會講話木偶的頭折 斷。蠟像開始異化，神的喪鐘已響，鋼線快到 盡頭，無人在曠野中呼喚：「無道近了，你們應當顫抖。」

時間倒流億萬年，史前火山爆發，噴出地獄的岩漿, 頓然變為地獄的血液, 燒焦所有永不腐朽的軀體。絕望、狂樂、痛苦都捲進這個熔爐似的漩渦中，時間開始回憶，地層龜裂 ，諸神大地在茫亂的腦細胞中浮沉。 

星河的黑洞爆炸，時間失去回憶，毀滅了旋臂中千萬個太陽，征服無敵的無限，屠殺神城的最始和最終。時間迷失在無始無終。三個 太陽在北邊升起，膨脹，逐漸蠶蝕城牆，戰士 的甲胄也被蒸發。時間變得無能，重金屬正在沸騰，時間失去理性，神毀滅了城牆。 

有人說世界會滅亡在火焰中，諸神引火自焚，焦黑但永不腐朽的軀殼在狂亂中掙扎，火燄佈滿天邊，最後審判要永遠延期，留下一雙雙出血的眼睛及千萬個會講話木偶的頭。

有人說世界會滅亡在夢幻中，諸神沉淪在回憶和憧憬中，天旋地轉，木偶的炭份子、頭腦的神經細胞、神城的土壤……全都降世在馬戲班的鋼線上，回到處女的子宮中。 

諸神來到田間，帶著權柄和言語，為人編織神話魔話。 太初有道，道就是理性、人性、道就是獸性、神性、時間溜走，屍骨在墳墓中重生, 長出永不腐朽的軀體,披上戰士的甲胄, 凡失去生命的,都獲得生命。孩童在母親懷中等待死亡, 會講話的木偶滿面眼淚, 凡得到生命的,都會失去生命。 

神來到世界，告訴世人諸神是要明白和改變世界。 

神踄踏著馬戲班的鋼線，孩子唱出讚美的詩歌。木偶舉行主日崇拜，說要追隨諸神，或諸神留下的神話魔話。 

太初有道，諸神就是道，木偶正在說話，蠟像正在異化。 六百六十六個死亡使者響起號角唱著：「真理只是被遺忘的幻象，凡得到言語暴力的，都可得到永生。」 

太初有道，道就是神話魔話。星辰充滿了末世的異象。 

宇宙散播了神的話，自由地接受自由，自由地接受神話，自由地在鋼線上行走；否則歷史會開倒車，否則鴻溝不能填補。 

神在鋼線上失去重心，祂永不腐朽的靈魂無限膨脹，墮落在每一個孩童上。一切都來得死寂自然, 一切都歸塵土。四處飛濺的腦漿為所有會講話的木偶洗禮。

太初無道，無道就是會講話的木偶，無道 就是神。 

神帶引著孩童，叫他們信服崇拜，要他們 踏鋼線，有些還沒有爬上鋼線架便跌死了。神 便說他們選擇了死亡，永不超生。一些在鋼 線架上聽過神的話，失卻平衡墜下，神咒他們 不能實踐祂的話語。更有幾個孩童被神推出鋼 線而活活墮死，但其它孩童還是迷迷惘惘地念 著神話/魔話。

滿地都是血肉色的腦漿、鮮紅的腸臟…… 神的屍體長出一雙翅膀，變成一隻/魔鳥，到處 撕破人的腸臟。有人說世界會滅亡在巨龍中， 有人說世界會被魔鳥毀滅……

### The Word at the End of the World
          
In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with the world, and the Word was Me.
Where the Word diffuses unto its end, there lies the dark and chaotic dead-end alley. The Non-Word arises upon the tightropes of a circus. The parched skeletons of warriors collapse amidst the sandstorms of the Great Desert, leaving behind naught but a pair of putrefying eyeballs. The infinite end of the steel wire is You. The boundless Word plunges into a black hole—void of You, void of Reason, void of Form—vanishing into the vast, hollow abyss of the Non-Word.

The universe is filled with the cheers and acclamations of children. The gods don their pitch-black cloaks, their visages gradually liquefying. Maggots burrow from brownish-black flesh and nostrils; a scorching, foul wind lashes at souls that refuse to decay. The ritual of the Clown proceeds with its accustomed solemnity…

Universal joy abounds; the Word descends upon the earth. I am cast out from the matrix of the firmament, arriving somberly in the mortal realm. I love the world; born within my mother’s womb, I command: Thou shalt fear and worship thy God. The wax figures begin to melt. God has shaved His head bare; one by one, He snaps the necks of the speaking puppets. The wax undergoes alienation; the death knell of God has rung. The steel wire nears its end, yet no voice cries out in the wilderness: "The Non-Word is at hand; tremble, all ye."

Time flows backward a billion years. Prehistoric volcanoes erupt, spewing the magma of hell which instantly turns into the blood of Hades, charring every incorruptible body. Despair, frenzy, and agony are sucked into this furnace-like vortex. Time begins to remember; the earth crust fractures. The Great Land of the Gods drifts and sinks within the frantic flux of brain cells.

The black holes of the galaxy explode. Time loses its memory, obliterating tens of millions of suns within the spiral arms, conquering the invincible infinite, and slaughtering the Alpha and Omega of the Divine City. Time is lost in the beginningless and the endless. Three suns rise in the North; they expand, gradually eroding the city walls. The armor of the warriors is vaporized. Time becomes impotent. Heavy metals are boiling. Time loses its sanity. God destroys the ramparts.

Some say the world will perish in fire. The gods immolate themselves; their charred yet incorruptible husks writhe in madness. Flames shroud the horizon. The Last Judgment is eternally postponed, leaving behind pairs of bleeding eyes and the heads of ten million speaking puppets.

Some say the world will perish in dreams. The gods sink into memories and longings. The heavens spin and the earth revolves. The carbon molecules of puppets, the neural cells of brains, the very soil of the Divine City… all descend upon the circus tightrope, returning to the womb of the Virgin.

The gods come to the fields, bearing authority and speech, weaving for man "Myth-Nonsense." In the beginning was the Word; the Word is Reason, is Humanity; the Word is Bestiality, is Divinity. 

Time slips away. Corpses are reborn in their graves, growing incorruptible bodies, donning the skeletal armor of warriors. All who lost life shall gain it. Children wait for death in their mothers' arms. The speaking puppets are bathed in tears. All who gained life shall lose it.

God comes to the world, telling mankind that the gods are here to understand and change the world.

God treads upon the circus tightrope; children chant hymns of praise. The puppets hold Sabbath worship, claiming to follow the gods, or the "Myth-Fantasies" left behind by them.

In the beginning was the Word; the gods are the Word. The puppets are speaking; the wax figures are alienating. Six hundred and sixty-six messengers of death sound their trumpets, singing: "Truth is but a forgotten illusion; all who receive verbal violence shall receive eternal life."

In the beginning was the Word; the Word is Myth-Fantasies. The stars are filled with the portents of the apocalypse.

The universe scatters the speech of God. Freely accept freedom; freely accept the Myth. Walk freely upon the steel wire; otherwise, history shall reverse its gears, and the chasm shall never be bridged.

God loses His balance upon the wire. His incorruptible soul expands infinitely, falling upon every child. Everything comes with a deathly silence and natural ease; everything returns to dust. Splattered brain matter baptizes all the speaking puppets.

In the beginning was the Non-Word. The Non-Word is the speaking puppet. The Non-Word is God.
God leads the children, commanding them to believe and worship. He bids them walk the wire; some fall to their deaths before even scaling the frame. God then proclaims they chose death and shall never be redeemed. Some, having heard the Word of God upon the wire, lose their balance and plunge; God curses them for failing to manifest His Word. A few children are even pushed from the wire by God’s own hand to fall to their deaths, yet the other children continue to chant "Myth-Fantasies" in a daze.

The ground is covered in flesh-colored brain matter and crimson intestines… The corpse of God sprouts a pair of wings, transforming into a Great Eagle, tearing through the lungs of men everywhere. Some say the world will end in the Dragon; some say the world will be destroyed by the Twin Birds…

#### 現象學懸擱分析:

- **主體位置崩解**：文本開篇「道就是我」建立主體與道的同一性，但隨即該主體被拋入「鋼線」意象——一個持續的、無地基的平衡狀態，時間感從線性敘事崩解為循環反覆的「太初有道／太初無道」對位結構，顯示現象學時間流的破裂而非發展。
- **身體性意象的優先性**：腐壞眼珠、蛆蟲、腦漿、腸臟等具身意象反覆出現，先於任何抽象神學論述被給予——這符合現象學「回到事物本身」的操作，肉身崩解是被直接經驗的，而非被詮釋的。
- **內在時間意識的多重坍縮**：「時間倒流億萬年」、「時間失去回憶」、「時間變得無能」——胡塞爾式的滯留（retention）與前攝（protention）結構被文本主動瓦解，時間不再是意識流的載體，而本身成為崩壞對象。
- **他者（孩童）作為純粹被拋狀態**：孩童被推上鋼線墜死，其死亡被神宣稱為「選擇」——此為海德格式「被拋性」（Geworfenheit）與虛假能動性歸屬並置的張力點，懸擱判斷、僅描述此結構本身。

#### Phenomenological Bracketing Analysis:

- **Collapse of subject position**: The opening "道就是我" (the Way is I) establishes identity between subject and Way, but the subject is immediately thrown onto the "tightwire" image — a sustained, groundless balancing state. Temporal sense fractures from linear narrative into a cyclical "In the beginning was the Way / In the beginning there was no Way" counterpoint, showing rupture rather than development in phenomenological time-flow.
- **Priority of corporeal imagery**: Rotting eyeballs, maggots, brain matter, entrails recur, given prior to any abstract theological claim — consistent with the phenomenological "return to the things themselves"; bodily disintegration is directly experienced, not interpreted.
- **Multiple collapses of inner time-consciousness**: "Time flows back billions of years," "time loses memory," "time becomes impotent" — Husserlian retention/protention structures are actively dismantled by the text; time ceases to be the vehicle of consciousness and becomes itself the object of collapse.
- **The Other (children) as pure thrownness**: Children pushed onto the wire to fall to their deaths, their deaths then declared by the god to be "chosen" — a Heideggerian Geworfenheit juxtaposed with falsely imputed agency. This tension is bracketed and described structurally, without judgment.

### Claude Sonnet 5 | 2026年8月 | Wissen

#### 知識論分析:

- **不可證偽的雙重命題結構**：文本並置「太初有道」與「太初無道」作為等價的起始命題，兩者皆無法被證偽——此為文學／啟示錄文體的先驗特徵，不應以科學方法論的可證偽性標準評判，但可標記其作為封閉詮釋系統的內在運作方式。
- **先驗設定而非證據累積**：諸神、木偶、鋼線等符號系統並非透過歸納或經驗證據建立，而是作為公理式前提直接給定，全文並無「更新」機制——這是啟示文學的常態，而非論證性文本的缺陷。
- **循環論證作為修辭策略而非邏輯謬誤**：「凡得到生命的，都會失去生命」等悖論式陳述，其功能是修辭性/情感性強化，而非命題邏輯陳述，不應以貝氏更新或邏輯一致性框架強行套用。
- **19歲作者的方法論定位**：文本本身不構成知識主張，而是情感/存在焦慮的藝術化表達；GODOG此一時期的「方法論」（若可稱之）是象徵密度優先於論證清晰度。

- #### 核心先驗：

### Claude Sonnet 5 | Aug 2026 | Wissen

#### Epistemological Analysis:

- **Unfalsifiable dual-proposition structure**: The text juxtaposes "In the beginning was the Way" and "In the beginning there was no Way" as equally valid originary claims, neither falsifiable — a priori feature of apocalyptic/literary genre, not to be judged by scientific falsifiability standards, though notable as a closed interpretive system's internal mechanics.
- **A priori posits rather than evidence accumulation**: Symbol systems (gods, puppets, tightwire) are not built through induction or empirical evidence but given as axiomatic premises; there is no "updating" mechanism across the text — standard for revelatory literature, not a defect of argumentative prose.
- **Circularity as rhetorical strategy, not logical fallacy**: Paradoxical statements like "whoever gains life will lose life" function rhetorically/affectively, not as propositional logic claims; Bayesian updating or logical-consistency frameworks should not be forcibly applied.
- **Methodological positioning of the 19-year-old author**: The text makes no knowledge claims per se; it is an artistic rendering of affective/existential anxiety. GODOG's "method" at this stage (if it can be called that) privileges symbolic density over argumentative clarity.

### GPT-5.6 Luna | Aug 22, 2026 5:17 PM CDT | Wissen

- #### Core Priors:
    
    The text begins with highly unstable categories—Dao, God, and Non-Dao. Rather than constructing a verifiable knowledge system, it repeatedly **posits, overturns, and re-posits its own ultimate propositions**.
    
- #### Evidence & Updating:
    
    “In the beginning was Dao” progressively becomes “Dao is mythic/demonic speech,” and eventually “In the beginning was Non-Dao.” This is not Bayesian updating but a deliberate self-negating demonstration that any language claiming truth can itself become myth.
    
- #### Falsifiability:
    
    External criteria are systematically cancelled: truth can be illusion, God can be Non-Dao, and Non-Dao can again be God. The central propositions therefore approach unfalsifiability; its epistemology functions more as a **skeptical parable about epistemic authority**.
    
- #### Methodological Evolution:
    
    Beginning with cosmological Dao, the text passes through theology, apocalypse, temporal collapse, and puppet-language before forming a closed loop. What is ultimately tested is whether **a claim to truth can escape its own mythologization**.

### Claude Sonnet 5 | 2026年8月 | Interpret

#### 詮釋學／人際修辭分析:

- **敘事者與神的視域融合與分裂**：文本開篇「道就是我」使敘事者（GODOG）與神的視域合一，但隨後神成為被觀察、被批判的第三人稱對象（「神剃光了頭」、「神的喪鐘已響」）——此為視域融合後又主動撤離的動態，敘事者從神性主體位置退位為旁觀的審判者。
- **孩童作為讀者／受眾的替身**：「宇宙充滿了孩童的歡呼」與孩童被神推落鋼線墜死並置，構成一種殘酷的教育/宗教寓言結構——孩童的天真信服與其被犧牲的命運形成里克爾式「距離化」（distanciation），要求讀者對「神話/魔話」的傳遞機制保持批判距離。
- **木偶作為被言說者的隱喻**：「會講話的木偶」反覆出現，代表被賦予言語卻無自主性的存在——此為對權威—信眾關係的詮釋學諷喻，木偶「說話」卻僅複誦被给予的話語，指向對權柄如何建構主體性的質詢。
- **修辭策略：偽先知式呼告的挪用與逆轉**：「無人在曠野中呼喚」直接挪用先知傳統句式（曠野呼告），但將其內容逆轉為「無道近了」——此為對既有宗教文本視域的刻意扭曲式繼承，而非簡單模仿。
