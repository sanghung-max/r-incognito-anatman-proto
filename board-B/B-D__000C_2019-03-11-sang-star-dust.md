## 懸置 / epoché

My long-time friend Piggy used to tease me as dry as a  "documentary film"; herself as "cartoon" as she enjoyed being silly, my long time former secondary school senior BHK (her husband) as "educational film" as he would act as an "educator" and started to give us a serious lecture on any contemporaneous topics we talked about.  

In retrospect (with the writings in psyche vivisection as documentary evidence), I might have started "epoche" at that time.  Use of this bracketing approach might have started earlier when BHK asked me (Form 4) to document  the brutality of Tiger Mui.  I reported my experience as happened and he submitted my writing the one of the Brother Patrick (superintendent).  Besides writing poems and a variety of different essays, I did record my dreams for some time and had half of notebook of my dreams for analysis.  

The hallucinatory imageries and delusional thoughts in GODOG's essays came loosely from those dreams.  In addition to the writings from the observations of different personae.  I worked for Trade Media (publisher of Asian Sources - Electronics Component) as a reporter when I was a first year student in Hong Kong University.  

In my professional life, I learned naturalistic inquiry and ethnomethodological research methods through practicing those methods with the data collected from my wife's dissertation (1994-1995).  She was in the Department of Education Administration and went to collect data from a Japanese High School  for her field work.  

After my years at Sprint, I moved to Samsung Electronics and founded Product Experience Research Department in the US.  That was the time when I used mixed method research heavily and routinely.  Because of the time involved in coding qualitative data, I eventually offloaded qualitative research to the vendors so that I could effectively do the quantitative research which I found most usability vendors might not have enough competence todo any advanced analysis beyond descriptive statistics (or having any clues in statistical inference at all.)

## 詮釋 / interpret

This can be the most fragile part as it depends on the lived experience and socio-cultural environment of the reader.  For those readers who do not have the lived experience in the specific epoch of the text, it is far too easy to assume their own value system and impose, infer and conclude from their own interpretations.  I have done experiments with commercial LLMs online and found their analysis and interpretation inadequate at best and misleading or mistaken.  If a relatively unbiased LLM (prompted to provide critical and unbiased reviews) had those issues, humans coming to interpret those texts would likely to be bound by their horizon.

The same problems would also extend to the personae including 無我  when interpreting these essays through the lens of their lived experience.  By default, a self-critique on one's own interpretations might be needed.
