## 亂語 / text

### Yat-Sang Hung | Mar 7, 1977
### 遊大潭
**7th March, 1977**    A-D__0006 p.23

藍天白雲山湖色,  
鴉雀聲音遍自然  
風盡野草無事處,  
俯首眺望鯉魚門  
幽徑秋林枯葉地。  
閃爍生光輝滿塘銀。  
水流潺潺古典樂  
恬靜迷人金陽中

書於丁巳年正月十八日  
1977年3月7日

### Claude Sonnet 5 | 2026年9月 | Epoché

##### 現象學分析:

- 感官場域轉為漢語古典山水詩之典型多重疊加:視覺(藍天白雲、山湖色)、聽覺(鴉雀聲、水流潺潺)、通感隱喻(水聲喻為「古典樂」)於八句內密集鋪陳,構成飽和知覺場,與前三首英詩的單一感官主導形成對照。
- 身體姿態具體化為「俯首眺望」——由俯視(俯首)轉向遠眺(眺望鯉魚門),構成垂直與水平雙軸的知覺運動,非靜態凝視。
- 「風盡野草無事處」呈現一種道家式「無事」現象學——意識懸置於行動之外,純然接收自然顯現,近乎《莊子》式逍遙,非西方浪漫主義之激情爆發。
- 「恬靜迷人金陽中」以「恬靜」收束全篇情感軸線,與Ode/Nature兩詩之狂喜(ecstasy)形成鮮明對比,顯示同一作者於不同語言register下調動截然不同之情感基調。

## English

### Claude Sonnet 5 | Sep 2026 | Epoché

##### Phenomenological Analysis:

- The sensory field shifts into a classical Chinese landscape-poetry mode: sight (blue sky, white clouds, mountain-lake hues), sound (crow/bird calls, murmuring water), and synesthetic metaphor (water-sound as "classical music") stack densely across eight lines — a saturated perceptual field contrasting with the prior three English poems' single-sense dominance.
- Bodily posture is made explicit — "俯首眺望" (bowing the head, then gazing afar) — a dual vertical-horizontal perceptual motion toward Lei Yue Mun, not a static gaze.

- The closing "恬靜迷人" (tranquil and enchanting) anchors the poem's emotional register in stillness, sharply contrasting the Ode/Nature poems' ecstasy — the same author modulating entirely different affective registers across language and genre.

- This poem marks a major register/language shift — from English Romantic apostrophe (Ode, Nature) and English narrative (Guiding) into the classical Chinese landscape-poetry (山水詩) tradition — evidence that the author was actively working within two distinct literary traditions simultaneously across 1976–77, not making an incidental language choice.

- 核心悖論:文本自身攜帶無法調和的時間標記衝突——「秋林枯葉」之季節能指與「正月十八日」之曆法能指互相矛盾,文本未提供任何後設說明(如「憶」「追記」等字樣)以縫合此斷裂,此一空白本身即德希達式「補充」(supplément)之匱缺,邀請讀者填補卻不提供依據。

- 「無事處」與「俯首眺望」之間存在張力:前者宣稱無為/無目的之在場,後者卻是高度目的性之視覺動作(眺望特定地標鯉魚門)——平滑(無事)與條紋化(眺望特定座標)空間邏輯並置卻未調和,呼應前三詩之反覆結構模式。
- 末句「金陽」與詩題「秋」意象再次交鋒——金色陽光可屬秋或春,此一曖昧未被消除,反而是文本唯一未被季節矛盾撕裂的意象,恰因其季節中立性。

## English

### Claude Sonnet 5 | Sep 2026 | Deconstruct

##### Post-Structural Analysis:

- Core aporia: the text carries an unreconciled temporal-marker conflict — the seasonal signifier "秋林枯葉" (autumn forest, withered leaves) contradicts the calendrical signifier "正月十八日" (18th day of the first lunar month), with no metatextual marker (e.g., "recalling," "recorded afterward") offered to suture the break. This gap is itself a Derridean supplément-absence — inviting the reader to fill it without supplying grounds to do so.
- The dual-calendar juxtaposition (lunar/Gregorian) is a trace of cultural hybridity — colonial Hong Kong's double temporal framework coexisting within a single colophon, neither purely traditional nor purely Westernized. This juxtaposition is never problematized by the text itself, only naturalized — exposing an unreflected cultural dual-track condition.
- Tension exists between "無事處" (a place of no affairs/purposelessness) and "俯首眺望" (bowing then gazing afar toward a specific landmark) — the former claims purposeless presence, the latter is a highly directed visual act (gazing at the specific landmark Lei Yue Mun). Smooth (purposelessness) and striated (targeted coordinate-gazing) spatial logics sit juxtaposed, unreconciled — echoing the recurring structural pattern across the prior three poems.
- The closing "金陽" (golden sun) resurfaces in tension with the poem's "秋" (autumn) imagery — golden light could belong to either autumn or spring, and this ambiguity is never resolved; it is, notably, the only image in the poem left untorn by the seasonal contradiction, precisely because of its seasonal neutrality.
