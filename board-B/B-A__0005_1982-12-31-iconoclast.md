### R. Incognito | circa Late Fall 1982  

#### Portrait of an Iconoclast & Random Notes

**
A-D__0008 p. 2-14

Lend me your eyes Students, Marxists, Christians, administrators, activists. I must admit that I am no sage by myself. I am but a fool,  a fool unlike those who who talk about their ideologies glibly, or else express themselves adequately. By an unnatural twists of dialectics, I would maintain. that vices are virtues and virtues are vices I would talk of something - that something makes people cry, laugh, die or drive their lives mad. Something that is so flickering and fictive as the mind. 

  

Man is a creature of finitude. His life is strung together  by a mosaic of meaningless fragments out of which man creates its meaning. Human life is but an absurd drama.

  

Brother and sister, can you spare a dime? Have you been stabbed on the back by someone you love? Have you wakened up with all your beloved or hatred turned to skeleton and skull? I cannot trust any human creation or contrivance We are constantly betraying and betrayed. What is man then? Man is then a hypocrite from within, a puppet from without.  No shame to those hypocrites - for they are the true rulers of our kind.  Hypocrisy is a vice as well as a virtue.I cannot imagine the world without hypocrisy.  For man and woman, I know too well to have myself understood.

  

I would ague that all religions, politics, science and morality are but rationalizations and justifications of human naivetes and irrationalities. Marxism, Christianity or even democracy are but the myths of the times. Pieces of intellectual absurdities (I would say garbage) men endorse.  History proceeds without laws.  Man is neither necessary nor sufficient. Uncertainty is the lord of control.

  

Devotion to a a certain form of ideology, or God or ideals of whatever kind is a form of self deception.  It works good for people.  They need it.  They cherish it.  They live up to it.   They praise it.  They curse it. They fight and die for it.  There may well be brilliant martyrs who sacrifice their lives to validate their own beliefs,  to validate their own selves.  But the very moment and acts of this self-validation also leads to self destruction. Self-sacrifice is suicide turned inside out. Iit is still a kind of suicide. Devotion carries with its own seed of destruction.

  
  

However, beliefs are also always divorce it from actions either intentionally or unconsciously.  It makes no difference.  It is also a form of self deception. I know that I am using a double bind - self deception is inescapable; no matter how we live. 

  

My mind is sick simply because I cannot accept or believe self deception.  I need self deception. I always want it. To live is to deceive oneself.  

  

There in mo humanity as such. Man is a deplorable animal.   He needs control. .He can’t be respected for he does not know what is respect.  Autonomy, freedom or self-determination are just contrivances/devices to justify man’s deeds.  What is it in their hearts?
