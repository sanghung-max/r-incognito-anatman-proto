## 亂語 / text

### R. Incognito | Jan 30, 2026
#### Who would like to be remembered as?
Reply to daughter's "Dad Book" prompt...  Jan 30, 2026

**
Nobody.  I am a nameless wanderer/drifter through time.  Everyday is a fresh day and I have no memory of the past.  I am not I am.  Thus one of my pseudonyms is Rover Incognito.  I don't need to be remembered for better or worse.  Meaning of life or existence is always created afresh at every instant.  The void, the nothingness is the center of my finitude.  It is also the infinite, stretching from the beginning of time to infinity. 

Instead of remembrance, incarnate, incorporate and invigorate the better values, the higher purpose beyond “who I am” you discover into your stream of life and carry forward beyond your stream of life.   

Partake and detach simultaneously; Introspect and observe both inward and outward world; Live by the moment and every moment - mindful but always hurl into the imaginary future.  Be prepared to return to the void or nothingness at any time.  

Inflated or deflated egos are a dime a dozen.  They are frequently sources of conflicts, sadness and suffering.  Life is too short to be dragged down by vicissitudes or successes of the past.  

So much for the almost senseless monologue.  To reiterate,  I don't need to be remembered as a person.  The meaningful “memory” is to become part of the embodiment of the greater purposes,  goals and dreams beyond who I am as a “person” - to understand, incarnate and invigorate without emotional turmoil; to overcome the burden of ego.


**
### Afterthoughts  August 23  2026

Having brought them into this world, 無我 has the commitment and responsibility to let all the personae known to them, for better or worse. Reading through the life history of all the personae and 無我 with *Verstehen* would be next to impossible without an intentional project.  Although they have come to know *R. Incognito* and *GODOG* since 2016, they have read very few pieces of those writings.  Reading through some of those writings may exact heavy emotional,  psychological and spiritual toll if they are not prepared.

The "retirement plan" from 無我 was to do immersive astrophotography and cosmology/physics studies as these were (and still are) the "ideal" state of being, forgetting past professional and personae lives.  However, for various reasons including 無我 daughter's projects of "Dad's" and "Mom's" Books, the Pandora's Box has been reopened and the personae are having a joyful (and painful) mutiny and escape.  

Life is a paradox - "no identity" is by itself an identity.  To 無我, It is an undecidable riddle not unlike that those Gödel or Russell had addressed in Mathematics and Logic (forgive my very plebeian interpretations).  Certainly, it is a metaphysical position, but it is 無我 ontological position.  Don't ask why - but 無我 is no armchair philosopher (or for any identity assumed).   This is a myth creation, preservation and destruction exercise.  Deconstructing the myth of  無我  -  Just play the painful game of life in a fictive but serious way.

## Chinese

### Claude Sonnet 5 | 2026年8月 | Deconstruct

- 核心悖論已由文本自行標舉:「無身分本身即是一種身分」——「無我」作為能指,其運作依賴於對「有我」的差異性標定(différance),故其自我消解的姿態恰恰是其身分穩定性的來源;文本承認此為「無法判定的謎題」而非欲解決的問題。
- 「Rover Incognito」之名本身構成延異鏈上的滑動:漫遊者(Rover)/隱姓者(Incognito)二詞互相取消定位座標——漫遊需要軌跡以被辨識為漫遊,隱姓卻抹除軌跡的可辨識性,能指在此自我抵銷卻仍持續運作、產生文本。
- 平滑空間(smooth space,無記憶、每刻嶄新)與條紋空間(striated space,「爸爸之書」計畫、女兒的敘事期待、生命史考掘)之間的張力未被解決,反而由「重啟潘朵拉盒子」一語暴露:條紋空間(家庭檔案、責任)強行侵入平滑空間(無我的存有論理想),而文本並未掩飾此侵入的不情願性("various reasons")。
- 缺失的痕跡(missing trace):文本未言明是何種「various reasons」促使檔案重啟,此空白本身即是解構閱讀的施力點——被壓抑或未明說之物,結構性地決定了整個計畫的觸發但拒絕現身。

### Perplexity | 2026 年 8 月 | Deconstruct

- 「Nobody」拒絕姓名與紀念，卻以 Rover Incognito／無我署名、發言並留存文本；無身份並非身份的外部，而是靠可重複書寫、被閱讀與被傳遞的痕跡才得以成立。
    
- 「每瞬重造」與「沒有過去記憶」被後記的日期、女兒計畫、2016 年相識史及待讀文稿穿透：當下已含有過去的殘留與未來讀者的預支，故遺忘／記憶、抽離／承擔無法二分。
    
- 「退休」中的天文攝影與宇宙學構成平滑的、去人格化的逃逸空間；「Dad／Mom’s Books」則把生命史重新條列、歸檔與可讀化。然而兩者互相滲透：檔案釋放人格面具的「快樂叛變」，逃逸反而生成更多敘事。
    
- 「無我是一個身份」及 Gödel／Russell 比擬標示文本的自指裂縫：若無我宣稱自身，便不能完全抹除主體；其「不可判定」不是待解的錯誤，而是保存、毀壞與再造神話得以反覆運行的條件。

### Perplexity | Aug 2026 | Deconstruct
