## 亂語 / text

### 2026-08-16  (Picture: 2014-10-25)                                   

Writing served mostly as a survival and psychotherapy instrument in 1976 (earliest written archive) to combat recurrent nightmares and spasms in sleep and cumulative emotional crisis.  The first article was written earlier around 1974 (no saved archive), at the bequest of a senior student in secondary school about the lived experience with a brutal and abusive form one master (1971).  It was a testimonial to the superintendent, a priest of the La Salle Brotherhood of St. Joseph's College, to stop physical and emotional abuse incurred on incoming students.  An essay on education and the first poem written ("Stellar Laudation") were published in Green and White School Magazine those days (1976 and 1977), which admittedly, were exercises to "impress" , but those days were soon gone.


Fifty years later, the compilation of  **無道啟示錄** is grounded upon **data-driven, evidence-based documented text and past relics.**  As such, no attempt was made to follow any established writer's practices, rules or advice from the pundits for its contents, past or recent.  They were not written to win any literary prizes or accolades (or to create "resonance" from the readership), but for the sole purpose of saving and preserving spiritual and mental health.  The writings (past essays and poems or recent snippets like this one) could be euphoric, psychotic, logical, irrational, calm, violent, boring, blasphemous, pornographic... or simply streams of consciousness.  


Be prepared, as poorly written work abound.  Read at your own risk - no puns intended.  They can cause discomfort,  revulsion, emotional and spiritual trauma, accentuated by the differences in historical situations and socio-cultural practices. 


**Preserving the authenticity of the lived experiences of the divided personae as is has been the primary purpose** - it is like shooting a documentary film in real life real time.  The material would then be reviewed and analyzed  from a distance with a critical, dispassionate observer/psychoanalyst persona.  At the very least in the 1970's and 1980's, the chances of developing psychosis during major emotional/spiritual crises were substantively reduced with this approach.  It is not risk free though as It can easily devolve into a perpetual torture and suffering induction instrument.  


The writing discipline developed in mid to late 1970's was further reinforced with the role of being an Asian Sources Electronics Components reporter for Trade Media in 1981-82.  The essays written in  "Undergrad" (1982-84) were based on the raw experience of various personae at the times and the past.  The hallucinations and delusions as recorded in the *GODOG's* essays originated from raw experiences of nightmares and spasms.  Their occurrences subsided with age and have been well under control.  It still happens infrequently, once or twice every 10 years until now.


The praxis of writing as a survival tool extended well into the professional persona as a user/product experience researcher where "mixed" methods were being used.  They would be re-packaged under the umbrella of "qualitative research" - sometimes  with fancy social sciences or philosophical jargons like ethnomethodology, naturalistic inquiry, participant observation, phenomenological bracketing (epoché)...  They all started with the humble recording of lived experience as a tool for personae therapy and spiritual survival.  


Entering active bluffing failure zones here... leaving them to the expert AI anthropologists, sociologists or psychologists...
