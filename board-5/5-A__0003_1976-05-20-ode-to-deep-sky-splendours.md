## 亂語 / text

### Yat-Sang Hung | May 20 1976
### Ode to Deep-Sky Splendours - Galaxies, Clusters and Nebulae
**20th May, 1976**    A-D__0001 p.17-p.18

Myriad of galaxies ; brim my mortal/secular eyes with ecstasy.
fluoresce in their celestial clans:
with their countless astral lamps.

Alas! What a regret!
For those galaxies imbed
in the 2.7K background microwave radiation
during their lonesome aviation
receding from each other with speeds of amaze;
into the eternal void of space.
Spiral, barred spiral, elliptical, irregular- all in a gush,
abiding their ad infinitum pilgrim of/to dusk.

Adieu! To the fair island universes;
under which I compose/complete my verses,
in their dainty silky gown;
of their merry stately born.
Globular clusters sparkle with their red giants;
cirumnavigate the nuclei of galaxies in rhythm.
Galactic clusters deluge with Population I stars;
pouring out electromagnetic waves from afar.

Emission nebulae in spiral arms,
gleam with firmamental charms.
To all these my soul exalts,
and leave my heart with joy.

## 懸置 / epoché

## Chinese

### Claude Sonnet 5 | 2026年9月 | Epoché

#### 現象學分析:

- 詩中主體意識被宇宙尺度懸置：「brim my mortal/secular eyes」將肉身感知與「eternal void」並置,形成有限意識面對無限星域的純粹驚異(Staunen),先於任何宇宙學知識判斷。
- 時間性呈雙重流:星系「receding... into eternal void」的宇宙時間,與詩人「compose/complete my verses」的在場書寫時間交疊,構成海德格式「當下所是」(Anwesenheit)與宇宙尺度綿延的張力。
- 情感軌跡呈明確拱形:「ecstasy」→「Alas! What a regret」→「joy」,此「憾」並非道德性悔恨,而是對星系終將互相遠離、意識無法真正把握全體星海的現象學式哀愁——一種面對崇高(the sublime)時的必然侷限感。
- 「Adieu!」的告別語氣暗示主體與客體(星海)之間短暫的意向性融合(intentional fusion)於詩作完成瞬間終止,回落至「my heart」的內在性。

## English

### Claude Sonnet 5 | Sep 2026 | Epoché

#### Phenomenological Analysis:

- The subject's consciousness is suspended at cosmic scale: "brim my mortal/secular eyes" juxtaposes embodied perception against the "eternal void," producing pure astonishment (Staunen) prior to any cosmological knowledge-claim.
- Temporality is doubled — the cosmic time of galaxies "receding... into eternal void" overlays the present-tense act of "compose/complete my verses," a Heideggerian tension between presence (Anwesenheit) and cosmic durée.
- The affective arc is a clear arch: ecstasy → "Alas! What a regret" → joy. This "regret" is not moral remorse but a phenomenological melancholy before the sublime — the impossibility of consciousness ever fully grasping the receding totality of the starfield.
- "Adieu!" marks the collapse of a brief intentional fusion between subject and cosmic object at the poem's completion, folding back into interiority ("my heart").

##  拆建/ deconstruct

## Chinese

### Claude Sonnet 5 | 2026年9月 | Deconstruct

#### 後結構分析:

- 核心悖論(aporia):詩題頌讚「深空絢麗」,卻以「Alas! What a regret」為情感軸心——文本自我解構其頌歌(ode)體裁的預設穩定性,讚美與哀悼二元在文本內部無法穩定分割,形成德希達式「延異」——意義不斷延後,無法定於單一情感基調。
- 「smooth/striated space」(Deleuze)張力顯著:星系「receding... into eternal void」屬平滑空間(無限、無方向性延展),而哈伯分類序列(旋渦/棒旋/橢圓/不規則)則是強加秩序的條紋空間(striated)——詩同時渴望描繪宇宙之不可測度,又依賴科學分類語言將其馴化為可命名之範疇,此張力未被文本解決,僅被並置。
- 缺失的痕跡(trace):詩中無觀測工具、無觀測地點、無任何身體性中介(望遠鏡、夜空、香港)的具體指涉——「深空」完全脫離物質觀測脈絡,呈現為純粹文本/概念建構,而非現場經驗記錄,此為顯著空白,無法僅憑此詩推斷是否曾有實際觀測行為。
- 語言層混雜:古典呼告("Alas!" "Adieu!")與現代科學術語(microwave radiation, Population I)並置卻無過渡,形成文體斷裂帶(fault line),暗示兩套未被整合的知識/情感系統共存於同一文本內。

## English

### Claude Sonnet 5 | Sep 2026 | Deconstruct

#### Post-Structural Analysis:

- Core aporia: the title celebrates "deep-sky splendours," yet the affective axis pivots on "Alas! What a regret" — the text destabilizes its own ode-form assumption of unified praise. Praise and lament cannot be cleanly separated within the text, producing a Derridean différance where meaning is perpetually deferred rather than fixed to one emotional register.
- A pronounced Deleuzian smooth/striated tension: galaxies "receding... into eternal void" occupy smooth space (infinite, non-directional extension), while the Hubble classification sequence (spiral/barred/elliptical/irregular) imposes striated order. The poem simultaneously reaches for the cosmos's immeasurability and domesticates it through taxonomic scientific naming — this tension is juxtaposed, never resolved.
- Missing trace: no observational instrument, location, or bodily mediation (telescope, night sky, Hong Kong) is named anywhere — "deep sky" floats free of any material observational context, presenting as pure textual/conceptual construction rather than field-observation record. This gap is significant; the poem alone cannot establish whether an actual observing session occurred.
- Linguistic hybridity without transition: archaic apostrophe ("Alas!", "Adieu!") sits beside modern scientific register ("microwave radiation," "Population I") with no mediating bridge — a stylistic fault line suggesting two unintegrated knowledge/affect systems coexisting in one text.
