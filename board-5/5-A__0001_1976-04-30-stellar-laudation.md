## 亂語 / text

### Yat-Sang Hung | Apr 22 1976
### The Stellar Laudation        
April, 1976   Yat   A-D__0001 p.13-14

#### 1
The leaves rustle on the sycamore trees,
swaying gracefully in the evening breeze.
The Sun sinks gloriously in effulgent hues,
touching the ocean with her last kiss.
Under the exquisite earthshine I will feast;
a sublime feeling overwhelms my cup of life.
The sky is resplendent with stars in peace,
after the lonesome foggy nights cease.

#### 2
Oh! Myriads of star-dusts in the Milky Way,
shine with heavenly grace..
How I rejoice with you!
With your charming smile and your superb style!
So pure and chaste!
A pleasure to my mind.
Living under the starry nights,
with a feeling sublimes,
a bliss I find!
In Desert you will be my guide;
In Chaos you will be my heart;
In peace you will be my life! 


#### 3
Oh! Stars, stars of love and hope,
spangle pellucidly in my soul,
where I quest and endeavor,
under the celestial fire,
illuminating my murky read.
Within your wondrous whole,
I live and grow;
striving for my ideals.
Yet my life is lackadaisical, 

#### 4
in my lonesome journey towards my goals,
plaintive for communion and warmth;
to enhance my zest for life,
and to keep up my quest for Truth! 


#### 5
There lies the beauteous reflection of the Milky Way,
the image of which sparkles dreamily,
on the undulating water.
How I long to touch you!
Whenever I find myself in despair;
you furnish me with hope and peace,
in your starry eyes.
Fill my bounded mind.
