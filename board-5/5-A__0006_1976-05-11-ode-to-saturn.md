## 亂語 / text

### Ode to Saturn
**11th May, 1976**    p.5 - p.6

Search!
and there she is, drifting with the stars,
effulges in her luster,
in the constellation Gemini.
The vault of heaven is sprinkling with star-dusts.
The atmosphere is steady and diaphanous.

Seek!
In the telescope,
the picturesque Saturn reveals;
in my earthly eyes, with the 6mm eyepiece,
showing blissfully,
of her unique peaceful style,
in her Golden Age,
Cassini Division is beautifully separated,
between Ring A and B.
The delicate crepe ring, irradiates subtly,
in its brownish tint.

Look!
in the disc,
the elegant gradation of colors,
from light yellow to brown (sorrel),
and the well-defined shadow of the rings.
Oh! Saturn, the planet of beauty,
Thee fills me with aesthetic pleasure.
