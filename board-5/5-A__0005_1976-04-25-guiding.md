## 亂語 / text

### Guiding
**25th April, 1976**    p.20

I stop quietly at the observing site;
setting my telescope by my side;
in a clear moonless night.
Soon stars appear in twilight;
I clamp my astrograph on the scope tight;
inserting an illuminated eyepiece & have my star field right.

With shutter open I start to guide;
thinking of the exquisite photos I can find,
I turn the cable all the time.
My neck is aching and my head in tired.
In summer, the mosquitoes are large & vile,
itching everywhere on the skin of mine.
In winter, the cold wind is bitter and wild,
slapping on my face hard and benumbing my mind,
but I must keep my guide star in align,
for the happiness I will find.
