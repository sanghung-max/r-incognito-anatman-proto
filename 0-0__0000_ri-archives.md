## 觀景 / vista


The initiation of this digital archival project marked the 50th Anniversary since the "undivided" self started to mutate to different personae back in 1976.  It is a serendipitous project (or an act of Zemblanity) of the reading of surviving archives after all these years.  It happened at this particular historical juncture with the stars and planets "aligned" (aka myth constructed from fata morgana).  

This is a broken instance where paradoxes collide, congeal and dissipate to nothingness, from affirmation of selves to their annihilation.

This archive should be read as a myth creation and myth deconstruction exercise for those who choose to engage.  Although it is claimed to be data-driven and evidence-based, elements reconstructed from memory rather than written records exist to fill in gaps and lapses in documentation.     

Because of the expanse of the contents, different LLMs have been used for "epoché", "wissen", "interpret", "deconstruct", and "ai-experts" unless otherwise stated.  Output generated were reviewed and selected with using and rotating the output of 1 or 2 current commercial LLMs from the following: Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, ChatGPT and Grok.  Selection will be determined by the quality and accuracy of output compared to the lived experience and may be subject to bias and other factors (e.g., availability) 

As it is a myth-creation machine, readers can also destroy the myths and return the state of the machine to Nothingness by uninstalling the  無道啟示錄 (progressive web application) if downloaded and installed .  Please refer to browser documentation for installation and availability of 無道啟示錄 offline.
