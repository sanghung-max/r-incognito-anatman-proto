## 亂語 / text

### Voice of Love / Affection
**10th April, 1976**    p.9

In my little selfish world,
with a noble feeling that sublimes,
pure, sincere and always true,
through which ever melts my heart.
For truth and happiness it pursues, 
blazing (burning) fiery in my soul, 
of faith and hope and my beauteous star,
devoted to your/thine ultimate good.
