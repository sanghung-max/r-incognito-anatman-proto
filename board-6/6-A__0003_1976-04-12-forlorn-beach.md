## 亂語 / text

### The forlorn beach
**12th April, 1976**    p.10

I take a stroll by the forlorn beach;
enjying the cool evening breeze.
A lonesome seagull catches my eyes,
shrieking desperately in his cry,
I sit on the undulating sand,
witnessing the exquisite sumset.
with folded arms and thinking deep,
pining for the one I need.
