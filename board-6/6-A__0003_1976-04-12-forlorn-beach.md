## 亂語 / text

### The forlorn beach
**12th April, 1976**    p.10

I take a stroll by the forlorn beach;
enjoying the cool evening breeze.
A lonesome seagull catches my eyes,
shrieking desperately in his cry,
I sit on the undulating sand,
witnessing the exquisite sunset.
with folded arms and thinking deep,
pining for the one I need.

## Chinese

### Claude Sonnet 5 | 2026年9月 | Epoché

#### 現象學分析:

- 時間結構呈現雙重節奏:「漫步」與「坐」標記出外部行動的停頓,對應內在凝視從動態轉向靜止的推移,黃昏(sunset)作為時間界限,象徵一日將盡的臨界意識狀態。
- 感官場域以聽覺(海鷗哀鳴)與視覺(起伏沙灘、絢麗日落)交織構成,「forlorn」一詞將主體的情緒直接投射於外在景物,呈現胡塞爾式的意向性——海灘本身不具荒涼性,荒涼是意識賦予的。
- 海鷗的「絕望鳴叫」functions as他者性(alterity)的短暫顯現,隨即被收回至主體獨白("folded arms and thinking deep"),顯示現象場最終仍收攏於自我封閉的沉思姿態。
- 結尾"pining for the one I need"揭示整個現象場——海、風、鳥、日落——皆是缺席客體的替代性填充,懸置(bracket)其真實所指後,詩本身即是一場對缺席的現象學描摹。

## English

### Claude Sonnet 5 | Sep 2026 | Epoché

#### Phenomenological Analysis:

- The temporal structure moves through two registers of action — walking, then sitting — mirroring a shift from outward movement to inward stillness, with sunset marking a liminal threshold of dwindling awareness.
- The sensory field interlaces the auditory (the gull's cry) and visual (undulating sand, exquisite sunset); "forlorn" projects the subject's affect directly onto the landscape — a Husserlian intentionality where desolation is constituted by consciousness, not inherent to the beach itself.
- The gull's "desperate" cry functions as a brief eruption of alterity, quickly reabsorbed into solitary interiority ("folded arms and thinking deep"), showing the phenomenal field collapsing back into self-enclosed reverie.
- The closing line reveals the entire scene — sea, wind, bird, sunset — as substitutive filler for an absent object; bracketed of referent, the poem is itself a phenomenology of absence.

##  拆建/ deconstruct

## Chinese

### Claude Sonnet 5 | 2026年9月 | Deconstruct

#### 解構分析:

- 核心悖論在於「孤獨」的修辭矛盾:文本標題與意象系統反覆強調孤寂(forlorn、lonesome),但整首詩的動機性結構卻是「尋求連結」(pining for the one I need)——荒涼場景實為對缺席他者的召喚裝置,而非真正的孤獨狀態本身,「forlorn」一詞因而自我解構。
- 「seagull」作為漂浮能指(floating signifier):牠既是被觀察之客體,亦透過「shrieking desperately」被賦予主體性投射,模糊了主體/客體之界線——讀者無法確定此「絕望」屬於鳥抑或屬於觀者自身,此為文本中最明顯的縫隙(aporia)。
- 空間上呈現平滑(smooth)與條紋化(striated)空間的張力:「undulating sand」為無固定形式之流動地景,與詩行本身固定的音步(iambic-like)格律及既定意象詞彙庫形成對抗——情感的流動性被強行收編進程式化的格律容器。
- 文本中不存在的「the one」構成最大缺席痕跡(trace):其身分、性別、關係性質全未被書寫,此空白本身即是全詩張力的結構性支點,任何填補皆是讀者投射而非文本內證。

## English

### Claude Sonnet 5 | Sep 2026 | Deconstruct

#### Post-Structural Analysis:

- The central paradox is a rhetorical contradiction in "solitude" itself: the imagery insists on isolation (forlorn, lonesome), yet the poem's motivating structure is one of seeking connection ("pining for the one I need") — the desolate scene functions as an apparatus summoning an absent other, not solitude as such; "forlorn" thereby deconstructs itself.
- The seagull operates as a floating signifier — observed object, yet also invested with subjectivity via "shrieking desperately" — collapsing the subject/object boundary. Whether the "desperation" belongs to the bird or to the observer remains undecidable, marking the text's clearest aporia.
- Spatially, there's tension between smooth space (the "undulating," formless sand) and striated space (the poem's own fixed metrical/rhyme scaffolding and inherited imagery bank) — affective fluidity is forcibly channeled into a conventionalized formal container.
- The unnamed "one" constitutes the text's most significant trace of absence: identity, gender, and relational nature are all unwritten. This void is the structural fulcrum of the poem's entire tension — any filling-in is reader projection, not textual evidence.
