### 關永祥 洪逸生 | 1983
### 無謂的答辯:與國事故人君對話

**關永祥
洪逸生**

看罷國事故人君的來文——《關於關永祥和洪逸生的《香港五百萬人自決前途》的兩點商榷》(下文簡稱《商榷》),實在令我們失望和沮喪。這似乎是過份武斷地幾棍子就把我們打為「無的放矢」、「亂打棍子、盲罵瞎打,不是有知識的人所應做的」,我們實在受不起這頂帽子,希望國事故人君切勿過份情緒化,把一些道德、主觀感情的判斷包裝為理性的「 商椎」,更冀望國事故人君不要墮進了「心理防衛系統」(psychological defense mechanism)中的投射行為(projection),把一些不為自己所接受的感情 、思想等扣在別人身上,明明是自己在亂打棍子,卻以為別人在亂打棍子。

首先,對於《商榷》一文中對港人的政治文化和政治架構的限制的分析,我們是同意的。在未撰寫《 香港五百萬人自決前途》一文前,亦明白到這個建議的不切實際,但我們卻認為在這個歷史契機中,是更應該說出我們的理想,在主流的意見中帶出點點的異議。當然我們不得不承認這是一個夢——————————個不能實現的夢,不過我們只希冀《香港五百萬人自決前途》 一文可以在寂靜的校園中營造一些討論氣氛,使同學可以從其他角度來考慮一個切身的問題———————九七問題 ,擴闊大家思考的範圍。

誠然我們是在發夢,在發「港人自決前途」 :但說穿了,國事故人君與其他贊成「港人治港」的朋友,又何嘗不是在發夢。從實是求是的角度來看, 「一個由各工商、文化、宗教和社會團顧所組成的大聯盟」簡直是癡人說夢話。要知道這些組織都是以利盆來維繫的,組織與組織間存在着或大或小的利益矛盾;而它們各自所關注的利益更導致了不同的政治觀覺,有傾向於保守、維持現狀的,也有傾於愛國、 成回歸祖國的,試問它們又靠甚麼來維繫一起呢?',

「港人治港」這個夢,是一個漂亮的理想,但不竟還是一個夢。試想一羣沒有自決權力的人,不過是人家手中玩弄的泥團,任人塑捏,「港人」成了治理的工具罷了。「香港五百萬人自決前途」是一個空想的話,「港人治港」亦不過是一個更大的空想。試想我們不能自決前途,因為在我們背後有張牙舞爪的解放軍,就如槍桿子架在頭上,要作抉擇一般;但在同樣的情況下,「港人治港」亦不過是一個空洞的模式 ・「港人」成了北京的傀儡,誰又會拿自己的生命來開玩笑,不竟英雄不再!

既然大家都在做夢的話,請國事故人君容讓我們沉醉在我們的夢中,亦容許我們可以說出自己的夢。 每人有做夢的權利,也有說夢話的權利,因為難保一天夢境成氮。你有做「大聯盟」的夢的權利,我們亦當有做「自決前途」的夢的權利吧!

一個沒有理想的時代,是一個可悲的時代,人的行為如盲蠅亂飛一般,毫無方向;但一個有理想而沒有勇氣說出來的人,又何嘗不是一個可悲的人,他的生命不過是慾望的盲動,生存成空虛!我們本着這個原則,借《學苑》一偶來暢舒心底的話,比諾把理想抱棄,與現實妥協的應聲蟲,縱未必優,但亦必不會不及。雖然是一個「近乎空想」毫不實際的主張」, 比諸厚顏獻媚,為當權者敲响鑼鼓之徒,我們雖非甚麼有識之士,但亦寧取前者,而不屑於後者的無恥。 不知國事故人君同意否?使我們不能明白的是:對於國事故人君———————————個信仰民主自由的人一認為「 居民的意願應該享有優先位置」,但又「抱着實是求是的態度」,認為「香港未來的政治安排,在現時及可見的將來,是無法由本地居民來主宰」,是如何處理這個矛盾的?

在《香港五百萬人自決前途》一文中,我們指出 :「國家、民族,掛在我們心中,不過是一些空泛的名詞。我不相信,更加不會接受因為所謂「國家前途 』、『民族利益」,便可以捐棄一己的尊嚴。我可以決定以「國家前途」、「民族利益」為自己的抉擇導向,但却不可以以此來強壓在他人頭上。人的尊嚴, 乃建立於可以為自己來決定、爭取自己的需要,沒有其他人可以代替他自己來決定。假若有的話,這便是壓迫。」在這裡,我們是希望大家明白到,在香港前途的問題上的抉擇,是一個個人主觀的價值取向,而非一個客觀的標準。但在我們眼中所見,却不難看到滿空帽子亂飛,愛國、愛民族成了被絕對化了的唯一標準。抉擇是一個個人的問題,你有你的抉擇,我有我的抉擇,區區不過屠望諸位有識之士不要以自己的抉擇、價值取向來為其他人下一個道德的判斷。正如國事故人君所言:「在香港這樣自由的此會,對於香港未來的政治安排,每個人都有權據自己的價值標準作出抉擇」,我們的觀點亦正是如此。因此謹請國事故人君莫要無的放矢,徒費筆墨。

最後謹以國事故人君的一句話,回敬國事故人君: “罵人可以,但要有理。…………………不要亂打棍子,盲罵瞎打,不是有知識的人所應做的。”

## English

### Perplexity | Aug 2026 | Translation

#### A Pointless Rebuttal: In Dialogue with Guo Shi Gu Ren

**By Guan Yongxiang and Hong Yisheng**

After reading Guo Shi Gu Ren’s response—“Two Points for Discussion on Guan Yongxiang and Hong Yisheng’s ‘Five Million Hong Kongers Decide Their Own Future’” (hereinafter referred to as “Discussion”)—we are truly disappointed and dejected. It seems that with overly arbitrary strokes, we have been beaten down as “shooting without a target,” “indiscriminately attacking, blindly scolding, not what knowledgeable people should do.” We truly cannot bear this label. We hope Guo Shi Gu Ren will not be overly emotional, packaging moral and subjective emotional judgments as rational “discussion.” Furthermore, we hope Guo Shi Gu Ren will not fall into the “psychological defense mechanism” of projection, attributing feelings and thoughts that one cannot accept onto others—clearly being the one who is indiscriminately attacking, yet thinking others are doing so.

First, regarding the analysis in “Discussion” on the constraints of Hong Kongers’ political culture and political structure, we agree. Before writing “Five Million Hong Kongers Decide Their Own Future,” we also understood that this proposal was impractical. However, we believe that in this historical moment, it is even more necessary to speak out our ideals, to introduce a bit of dissent within mainstream opinion. Of course, we must admit this is a dream—an unachievable dream. But we only hope that our article can create some atmosphere for discussion in the silent campus, allowing fellow students to consider a pressing issue—the 1997 question—from other angles, broadening the scope of everyone’s thinking.

Admittedly, we are dreaming, dreaming of “Hong Kongers deciding their own future.” But to put it plainly, aren’t Guo Shi Gu Ren and other friends who support “Hong Kongers ruling Hong Kong” also dreaming? From a pragmatic, truth-seeking perspective, “a grand alliance composed of various industrial, commercial, cultural, religious, and social groups” is simply a fool’s daydream. One must know that these organizations are bound by interests; there exist major or minor contradictions of interest between organizations, and their respective concerns lead to different political perceptions—some lean toward conservatism and maintaining the status quo, others lean toward patriotism and supporting return to the motherland. I ask you, by what can they be bound together?

“Hong Kongers ruling Hong Kong” is a beautiful ideal, but it is still a dream. Just imagine: a group of people without the power of self-determination are merely mud in someone else’s hands, to be molded and shaped at will; “Hong Kongers” become merely tools of governance. If “Five Million Hong Kongers Decide Their Own Future” is a fantasy, then “Hong Kongers ruling Hong Kong” is nothing but an even grander fantasy. Just imagine: we cannot decide our own future because behind us stands the People’s Liberation Army with bared teeth and claws, like a gun barrel pointed at our heads, forcing a choice. But under the same circumstances, “Hong Kongers ruling Hong Kong” is also nothing but an empty model. “Hong Kongers” become puppets of Beijing; who would joke with their own life? Alas, heroes are no more!

Since everyone is dreaming, please, Guo Shi Gu Ren, allow us to indulge in our dream, and allow us to speak our dream. Everyone has the right to dream, and the right to speak dream-talk, because who knows if one day the dream may come true. You have the right to dream of a “grand alliance”; we likewise have the right to dream of “deciding our own future”!

An era without ideals is a tragic era; human behavior is like blind flies buzzing about, utterly directionless. But is not a person who has ideals yet lacks the courage to speak them also a tragic person? Their life is nothing but the blind movement of desires, existence become emptiness! Guided by this principle, we borrow a corner of _Xueyuan_ to speak our heart’s words. Compared to those who abandon ideals, compromise with reality, and become yes-men—even if we are not necessarily superior—we are certainly not inferior. Though ours is a “nearly fantastical,” utterly impractical proposition, compared to those who shamelessly flatter and beat drums and gongs for the rulers, though we are not what one would call “knowledgeable,” we would still prefer the former and disdain the latter’s shamelessness. I wonder if Guo Shi Gu Ren agrees?

What we cannot understand is this: Guo Shi Gu Ren, a believer in democracy and freedom, holds that “residents’ will should occupy a priority position,” yet “adopting a pragmatic, truth-seeking attitude,” also holds that “Hong Kong’s future political arrangements, at present and in the foreseeable future, cannot be controlled by local residents.” How is this contradiction to be resolved?

In our article “Five Million Hong Kongers Decide Their Own Future,” we stated: “Nation and ethnicity, hanging in our hearts, are merely empty terms. I do not believe, and furthermore will not accept, that one can abandon one’s own dignity for the so-called ‘national future’ or ‘national interests.’ I may decide to orient my choices by ‘national future’ or ‘national interests,’ but I cannot impose these on others. Human dignity is built on the ability of a person to decide and strive for their own needs; no one else can decide for them. If there is such a thing, it is oppression.” Here, we hope everyone understands that on the issue of Hong Kong’s future, choice is a matter of personal subjective value orientation, not an objective standard. But in our eyes, it is not difficult to see hats flying everywhere in the sky—patriotism and love of nation have become absolutized as the sole standard. Choice is a personal matter; you have your choice, I have mine. We merely hope that knowledgeable gentlemen will not use your own choices and value orientations to make moral judgments about others. As Guo Shi Gu Ren himself said: “In a free society like Hong Kong, regarding Hong Kong’s future political arrangements, everyone has the right to make choices according to their own value standards.” Our viewpoint is precisely this. Therefore, we respectfully request Guo Shi Gu Ren not to shoot without a target, wasting ink.

Finally, we respectfully return to Guo Shi Gu Ren one of his own words: “It is fine to scold people, but be reasoned… Do not indiscriminately attack, blindly scold; this is not what knowledgeable people should do.”

#### 現象學懸置與主體性流動

- 文本以「夢／說夢話」為核心懸置（epoché），將國事故人的「務實可行性」括號化，轉而聚焦「理想表達權」的主體性經驗，呈現一種由挫敗感（失望、沮喪）到抗辯性自持的內在情感流。
- 時間性上，從「歷史契機中說出理想」的當下行動，推進至「夢境成真」的未來開放性，再以「沒有理想的時代＝可悲」的規範判斷，形成由防禦到宣示的內在時間弧。
- 內部邏輯自洽：以「個人主觀價值取向≠客觀道德標準」為先驗前提，推導出「自決夢」與「大聯盟夢」同屬夢境，並拒斥任何以「務實」名義的價值優位性。

### Claude Sonnet 5 | Aug 2026 | Epoché

- 情感基調開篇即以「失望和沮喪」直陳受傷情緒,與前兩文的克制形成對比——本文是三篇中唯一將論辯對手的言語行為本身(而非其論點)標記為情感創傷來源的文本,情感流向由受挫、防衛轉向後段的悲愴自陳(「生存成空虛」)。
- 時間結構呈現一種「夢」的懸置時間性:非線性論證推移,而是在「承認不可行」與「仍要說出」之間反覆擺盪,構成一種不歸屬於過去/未來的、純粹當下發聲的現象學時刻——言說本身即是目的,不指向可實現的未來。
- 內在邏輯的存有論核心由「尊嚴—抉擇」轉移至「言說—做夢的權利」:純粹主體經驗不再是被剝奪的憤怒,而是自我防衛式的堅持——即使明知空想,仍主張「說夢話」本身具有存在論意義,拒絕以結果檢驗存在的正當性。
- 結尾以反問句直指對方(「不知國事故人君同意否?」)收束,情感基調維持在防衛與挑戰的張力之中,未達致和解。

## English

### Perplexity | Aug 2026 | Epoché

#### Phenomenological Bracketing and Subjective Temporality

- The text brackets Guo Shi Gu Ren’s “pragmatic feasibility” via an epoché of “dream/speaking dream,” foregrounding the subjective experience of “right to express ideals,” revealing an internal affective flow from frustration (“disappointed and dejected”) to defiant self-assertion.
- Temporally, it moves from the present act of “speaking ideals in this historical moment” to the future openness of “dreams may come true,” then to the normative judgment “an era without ideals is tragic,” structuring an internal arc from defense to proclamation.
- Its internal logic is coherent: starting from the a priori “personal subjective value orientation ≠ objective moral standard,” it derives that both the “self-determination dream” and the “grand alliance dream” are equally dreams, rejecting any value supremacy claimed under the banner of “pragmatism.”


### Claude Sonnet 5 | Aug 2026 | Epoché

- The affective register opens by directly naming "disappointment and dejection," in contrast to the restraint of the two prior texts — this is the only one of the three to mark the opponent's rhetorical conduct itself (not just his argument) as a source of emotional injury; affect moves from woundedness/defensiveness toward a mournful self-disclosure near the close ("existence becomes emptiness").
- Temporal structure enacts a suspended, dream-like temporality: not linear argumentative progression but oscillation between "conceding infeasibility" and "still insisting on speaking," producing a phenomenological moment belonging neither to past nor achievable future — speech itself is the end, not oriented toward a realizable outcome.
- The ontological core shifts from "dignity-as-agency" to "the right to speak/dream": pure subjective experience is no longer dispossessed anger but self-defensive persistence — insisting that "talking in one's sleep" has existential worth even when known to be futile, refusing to let outcome be the test of legitimacy.
- The closing rhetorical question directed at the opponent ("I wonder if 國事故人 agrees?") holds the affect in unresolved defensive tension, reaching no reconciliation.

#### 認識論先驗與證據更新

- 核心先驗：「個人主觀價值取向≠客觀道德標準」為不可證偽的道德公理；「夢有說出的權利」為表達自由的先驗前提，驅動全文對「務實 vs. 理想」的對等化。
- 證據更新機制：以「大聯盟＝利益矛盾組織的癡人說夢」「港人治港＝傀儡模式」為反證，削弱國事故人「務實方案較可行」的信念強度，將兩者同歸為「夢」。
- 方法論演化：從承認「自決不切實際」到主張「理想表達具歷史契機意義」，再以「夢境平等」消解可行性爭議，呈現一種非波普爾式的修辭等值策略：可行性讓位於表達正當性。

### Claude Sonnet 5 | Aug 2026 | Wissen

- 核心前提發生顯著證據更新:作者承認「在撰文前已明白這個建議的不切實際」,此為對原初立場的貝氏式修正——不可行性已被納入先驗知識,論證重心從「主張可行」轉移至「主張表達本身具正當性」,原命題的可證偽性條件因而改變(不再是「自決是否可行」,而是「言說是否有權」)。
- 對「大聯盟」提案採取對稱性證偽策略:以「組織間存在利益矛盾、政治傾向分歧」為經驗證據,指出此提案同樣不可行,此為方法論上的鏡像操作——用對手要求己方論證所受的相同「可行性」標準回敬對手,構成一種標準一致性的檢驗(若標準適用於甲論,亦應適用於乙論)。
- 對國事故人「居民意願優先」與「現實上無法自主」二者並存,作者指出此為未解決的邏輯矛盾,但並未提出反證或替代命題,僅止於指認矛盾本身,屬批判性質詰而非建設性證偽。
- 全文未見對己方原命題的近一步經驗檢驗或方法論精煉,證據運用集中於類比反擊(「你也在做夢」)而非累積新證據,顯示論辯策略從實證轉向修辭對等。

## English

### Perplexity | Aug 2026 | Wissen

#### Epistemic Priors and Belief Updating

- Core prior: “Personal subjective value orientation ≠ objective moral standard” is an unfalsifiable moral axiom; “the right to speak dreams” is an a priori of expressive freedom, driving the essay’s equalization of “pragmatism vs. ideals.”
- Evidence updating: counter-evidence such as “grand alliance = daydream of interest-conflicted organizations” and “Hong Kongers ruling Hong Kong = puppet model” weakens Guo’s belief that “pragmatic proposals are more feasible,” collapsing both into the category of “dreams.”
- Methodological evolution: moves from conceding “self-determination is impractical” to asserting “expressing ideals has historical moment significance,” then dissolves feasibility debate via “equality of dreams,” reflecting a non-Popperian rhetorical equivalence strategy: feasibility yields to expressive legitimacy.

### Claude Sonnet 5 | Aug 2026 | Wissen

- A significant evidence-update occurs in the core prior: the authors admit they "understood the proposal's impracticality before writing it" — a Bayesian revision incorporating infeasibility into prior knowledge, shifting the argument's center from "this is feasible" to "the right to voice it is legitimate regardless." The falsifiability conditions of the original claim thereby change — no longer "is self-determination feasible" but "is speech itself entitled."
- A symmetrical falsification strategy targets the "grand coalition" proposal: citing inter-organizational interest conflicts and divergent political leanings as empirical evidence of its equal infeasibility — a methodological mirroring, applying the same "feasibility" standard the opponent demanded of them back onto the opponent's own proposal, testing standard-consistency (if the standard applies to claim A, it must apply to claim B).
- On 國事故人's co-held positions — "residents' will should take priority" alongside "in reality, local residents cannot be self-governing" — the authors identify this as an unresolved logical contradiction but offer no counter-evidence or alternative proposition, stopping at naming the contradiction rather than constructively falsifying it.
- No further empirical testing or methodological refinement of the original claim appears; evidentiary use centers on analogical counter-attack ("you too are dreaming") rather than accumulating new evidence, indicating a shift in strategy from empirical argument to rhetorical parity.

## 詮釋 interpret

## Chinese

### Perplexity | Aug 2026 | Interpret

#### 詮釋視域融合與修辭人格

- 文本以「受委屈的理想主義者」視角，與國事故人的「務實批判者」視域形成對抗性對話，促成讀者從「可行性爭議」轉向「表達權利平等」的視域重構。
- 修辭策略上，使用反諷（「容讓我們沉醉在我們的夢中」）、隱喻（「盲蠅亂飛」「應聲蟲」）與直接引語回敬（「罵人可以，但要有理」）強化道德對等性，並藉「夢境平等」消解對方務實優位。
- 人格動態：從防禦性辯解（「受不起這頂帽子」）轉向宣示性抗辯（「寧取前者，不屑後者」），完成由被動受責到主動道德定位的詮釋轉折。

### Claude Sonnet 5 | Aug 2026 | Interpret
